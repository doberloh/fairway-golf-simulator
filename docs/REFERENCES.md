# Reference photographs

The coast redwood models in `vendor/grown-redwood-forest/` were shaped against 315
photographs of redwood and Pacific-Northwest forest, gathered from Wikimedia
Commons. This file is the record of what was looked at.

**None of them is in this repository and none of them was copied.** They were
downloaded to a scratch directory, measured for colour and structure, read
directly for shape, and left there. Every triangle in `grown-redwood-forest` is
generated by `tools/grow.mjs`; the photographs decided proportions, not pixels.

Reproduce the set with `tools/fetch-references.py`, which queries the Commons
API and keeps only files under a licence that permits reuse.

## What was gathered

| subject | images | what it settled |
|---|---|---|
| grove | 70 | whole-tree proportion, how much of a trunk is bare |
| understory | 99 | sword fern, salal, sorrel, the shape of the floor |
| deadwood | 37 | nurse logs, stumps, snags, and where moss sits on them |
| trunk | 34 | bark colour, fluting, the buttressed foot |
| canopy | 24 | foliage spray shape, and how dark a grove really is |
| hemlock | 19 | a lower, mid-heavy crown |
| fir | 17 | whorled tiers, and green increasing toward the ground |
| cedar | 15 | drooping sprays, stringy bark |

## After the ball lands (2 October 2026)
