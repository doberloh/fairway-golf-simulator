# Fairway 0.9.18 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.18`, published 2026-11-17.

A new way to swing: pull back and push through, with your thumb, the mouse or a controller's right stick.

**The swing pad.** The three-tap Swing Meter bar is gone. Press **Swing** and a small pad opens: pull your thumb (or the mouse) down below the gold line for the distance, then push back up through it to hit. The ruler down the side is marked in yards for the club in your hands, and the blue notch shows the pull that reaches your aim. Lift before you push and nothing happens, so you can take your time. On a controller the right stick does the same. Space no longer swings.

**Where the ball starts and how high it flies come from the swing.** Start your push left or right of the line and the ball starts that way. Where your thumb finishes picks the shot: out to the right for a Push, to the left for a Pull, straight on for Straight; just past the line for Low, further for Normal, up high for High. Draw and fade come from the spin ball, so a push with a full draw comes back to your aim.

**Rhythm counts.** A gold dot shows the pace of a good swing on Easy and Normal. Your push is timed against it. On Hard there is no dot, and your backswing is judged too: about three times as long as the push is right, like a real golf swing.

**What you hit.** After each shot the swing box shows the line, the start, the height and the spin, a tempo gauge, and then the carry and the total as the ball lands and stops. The pad shows the path your thumb took for a second after the shot, and again if you open it before your next one. Drag the pad by its top bar to wherever suits your hand; it remembers. On a phone it sits at the foot of the screen.

**Phones.** The main menu no longer scrolls sideways on a narrow phone, including with the keyboard up.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.17. The ball's flight, the scoring, the challenges and the course of the week are as they were. With a launch monitor the game plays exactly as before.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.17: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and friend challenge codes. A meter look you unlocked is kept but colours nothing for now, while we decide what the pad should wear.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
