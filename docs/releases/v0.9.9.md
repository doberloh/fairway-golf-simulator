# Fairway 0.9.9 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.9`, published 2026-11-08.

A small fix for courses made in the Course Builder.

**Greens mown to the edge.** On a course from the Course Builder, whether generated, traced from a map or built from LIDAR, the diamond mowing on a green stopped short of its edge and left a plain band inside the collar. The diamonds now run all the way out to the clean-up pass round the edge, as on every other course.

**Tees mown on built courses.** Tees on a Course Builder course had no mowing at all. They now have their bands along the line of play and the clean-up pass round the edge. Where a tee sits right beside another hole, a corner of it can take that hole's direction.

Nothing else changes. Your courses, saved rounds and settings carry over from 0.9.8 as they are, and friend challenge codes from 0.9.8 do not play on 0.9.9.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
