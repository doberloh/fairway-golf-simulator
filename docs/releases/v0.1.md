# Fairway 0.1 (beta)

Tag `v0.1`, published 2026-10-03.

The first beta of Fairway: a golf simulator with procedurally generated courses that runs in your browser, with or without a launch monitor.

**Download the zip for your computer:** Windows, Mac (Apple silicon or Intel) or Linux. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code.

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download: the whole game, no server needed.

This is a beta. It hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting. `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.
