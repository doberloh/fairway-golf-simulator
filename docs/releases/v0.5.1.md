# Fairway 0.5.1 (beta)

Tag `v0.5.1`, published 2026-10-09.

A small update to 0.5, with one new thing to see and one fix.

**Your score, in the middle of the screen.** The moment you finish a hole, its name comes up in the centre of the screen -- *Birdie*, *Par*, *Bogey*, all the way to *Quadruple bogey* -- and past that just the number over par, like **+5**. A hole in one is always a *Hole in one*. Under it are your strokes, the par and your score against par, green under and red over. It rises in, holds for a moment and lifts away before the scorecard opens. It works in every format: with a group it names the golfer, and in a scramble it names the team once the team's score is settled.

**The flag on the putting marker is the right way up.** On the green, when the cup is off the edge of the screen, the pin marker waits at the edge and points toward it. It used to turn the whole marker to do that, so with the cup behind you the flag hung upside down. Now a small arrow on its ring does the pointing and the flag stays upright.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code, and `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Your players, saved rounds and courses from 0.5 carry over, and every course grows exactly the same ground. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting.
