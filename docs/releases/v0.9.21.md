# Fairway 0.9.21 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.21`, published 2026-11-25.

Calmer grass on every course, and a Course Builder that draws smooth shapes instead of squares and takes on much bigger LIDAR downloads.

**The grass.** Every landscape's grass is a little darker and less neon, the rough most of all, so the course no longer washes out on a bright screen or a projector. Desert scrub and links fescue, already straw, keep their colour. Slopes still show as drier grass than the hollows, but now they turn gently toward straw instead of going lime-yellow, and a fairway on a steady hillside shows its steep and gentle parts rather than one even colour.

**Course Builder: no more squares.** Everything you trace, paint or bring in is kept as smooth outlines, the way the game draws it. The squares are gone from the trace, the review and the 3D editor.
- **Traced** surfaces (the automatic trace, Paint rock on slopes, and drafts saved by an older builder) come in as a smooth line through the old steps, with real corners, like a square tee's, kept sharp.
- **Painted** outlines are drawn finely, so Auto curve no longer leaves visible corners on a small green.
- **OpenStreetMap** greens, fairways, bunkers and paths come in as smooth curves through the map's points, with tees and car parks kept square.
- **Smooth** now really takes out waves and corners without shrinking shapes or cutting the end off a narrow path. Smoothing a whole layer keeps sharp corners; the Smooth brush rounds a corner you run it over.
- **Normalize width** lays paths along a smooth middle line.
- A course file saved by an older builder has its edges smoothed once when you open it in the 3D editor. A note says so, and Undo puts them back.

**Course Builder: bigger courses.**
- A LIDAR ZIP of any size opens, even several gigabytes. A ZIP of zLAS files, which the builder cannot read, is named as such, with where to convert them.
- Draw your course's rough outline in the LIDAR step: everything inside it gets the survey's full detail, and the land outside it less, which keeps a big area quick and light. Bunkers, tees and greens inside the outline get half-metre ground.
- Fill outside sizes its woods from the survey's own trees.
- Nothing fails silently: a file that cannot be read says why.

**Course Builder: smaller things.**
- **New tools:** a **Fringe** layer. A green has a fringe only where you paint one; courses made outside the builder keep theirs. Also a Line tool with square bends, houses of any size, clean-up boxes, *Add layer around*, *Outside the course*, and a courses folder to save into and open from.
- **Clearer screens:** tees drawn in their set's colour, and the next hole offered after you place a green. Every button and panel says what it does, and Fetch OpenStreetMap says what it is doing. Cart paths are dark grey.
- **In the game:** a built course's fairways are mown in one direction a piece.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.20, and a course file plays as it did. The ball's flight, the challenges, the course of the week and online play are as they were.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.20: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and builder drafts. **Everyone in an online room needs 0.9.21**, and a friend's challenge code is tied to the version it was made on, so make sure you both have 0.9.21.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
