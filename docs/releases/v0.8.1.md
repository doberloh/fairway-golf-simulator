# Fairway 0.8.1 (beta)

Tag `v0.8.1`, published 2026-10-16.

Skins becomes a way to play a whole round, Endless keeps its daylight, and rounds with a late arrival save properly again.

**Skins is a format now, not a challenge.** Choose it under **Play → Format** for two to four golfers: the lowest score alone wins each hole, ties carry the skins on, and the golfer with the most skins wins the round. **Challenges** now holds three games: closest to the pin, long drive, and up and down.

**Endless starts in the light you chose it in.** An Endless run started from the main menu no longer drops you onto the hole at midnight; it starts at the hour the menu was showing.

**Rounds with a late arrival save properly.** A round where someone joined partway through can be continued after closing the game again, and mulligans work in it.

**Behind the scenes.** Every format is now tested automatically with every group of one to four golfers, which is how the save problem was found.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

Everything from 0.8 carries over, and a round saved partway through the old Skins challenge continues as a skins round. A skins round saved in 0.8.1 won't open in 0.8. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or device.
