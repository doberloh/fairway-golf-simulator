# Fairway 0.9.13 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.13`, published 2026-11-12.

The ball, rebuilt: how it flies, lands, bounces, rolls and drops is all new, and checked against real measurements. Plus a gimme circle for putting.

**A new ball.** Every part of a shot was rebuilt from published measurements: tour launch monitor tables for flight, the golf bodies' own tests for how a ball lands on a green, and lab studies for putting and the hole. A drive now bounces four or five times before it runs. An iron pitches, hops and releases the way its spin and the green say it should. A chip checks or runs out depending on how it was struck.

**Three course conditions.** The old list of turf settings is now three, asked when you set up a round and changeable at any time: **green speed** (the Stimp reading, 6 to 15), **green firmness** and **fairway firmness** (Soft, Medium or Firm). There are ready-made choices too, including an **Everyday course** with slower greens that hold a shot, for anyone who does not want to play on tour greens.

**Wedges that spin.** Whether a wedge stops, hops on or comes back depends on its spin, how steeply it lands, and the green. A well-struck wedge stops near its mark on a soft green and can come back a few yards on a firm, fast one. From a launch monitor, the spin you hit is the spin the ball gets.

**The rough matters more.** Rough and semi-rough still cost ball speed, and now they also cost a lofted shot its spin, as grass between the face and the ball really does. A wedge from the rough lands and runs out instead of stopping. Long irons are not affected. Sand costs a little less speed than before.

**Putting and the hole.** Putts roll out to the distance the green speed says. The hole is now real: a ball can catch the lip, ride round it, drop, or run across the top if it arrives too fast, and the flagstick is a real stick it can hit.

**Putting with a launch monitor.** A real putt skids before it rolls and loses about three tenths of its speed doing it, and Fairway's putts do the same. On a level, medium-speed green a 10 ft putt needs about 5.3 mph of ball speed and a 20 ft putt about 8.1. Some simulators leave the skid out; a putt that is right there will be short here. A putt that leaves the face with a little loft no longer loses distance to its first hop, so it rolls further than it did in 0.9.12.

**Gimme circle.** A new putting choice: one circle round the hole, its size set by you from half a foot to 30 feet. A ball that stops on the green inside it is picked up for one more stroke. Outside it you putt out as usual.

**Your saves.** Courses, saved rounds, settings and friend challenge codes carry over from 0.9.12. An older save's turf settings are read into the three new conditions. The ball behaves differently now, so a shot replayed from an older friend's challenge will not land exactly where it did for them.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
