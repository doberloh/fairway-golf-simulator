# Fairway 0.8.2 (beta)

Tag `v0.8.2`, published 2026-10-16.

Thirty-six challenges, medals, and a challenge of the week.

**36 challenges.** Off the tee, approach shots, the range, the short game, putting, head-to-head games and rounds with a goal -- all open from the start, on the main menu under **Challenges**.

**Medals.** Each challenge has a bronze, silver and gold to earn, and you can play any of them again as often as you like.

**The Challenge Course.** Every challenge on a course plays on the same eighteen holes for everyone, with the balls in the same places, so a score means the same thing on every copy.

**Challenge of the week.** A new one every Monday, the same for everyone, with no account needed.

**Five new achievements** for your medals, the weekly challenge and head-to-head wins.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

Everything from 0.8.1 carries over, and your old challenge records count toward the new medals. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or device.
