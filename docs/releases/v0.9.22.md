# Fairway 0.9.22 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.22`, published 2026-11-26.

Turf that looks like one course: the fairway, fringe and green of every landscape are now the same grass cut to different heights, with soft edges between them, and you can choose how each is mown.

**The turf.**
- **One grass, cut to different heights.** On every landscape the semi-rough, fairway, approach, tee, fringe and green are now one shade of green, getting lighter as the grass gets shorter, with the green the lightest. Before, some landscapes had a green that was a different colour from its own fairway, and some were washed out. The rough keeps its own colour.
- **Seasons follow.** When spring, autumn or winter changes the fairway, the fringe and green change with it.
- **Soft edges.** Rough to semi-rough, semi-rough to fairway, fairway to fringe, fringe to green, the tees and the bunkers now blend over a few centimetres instead of meeting in a hard line. Bunkers keep their sandy lip. Where your ball lies is decided exactly as before.
- **Slopes and dry patches on mown grass** show as the same grass a little lighter and greyer, about half as strong as before, with no orange bands round raised greens. The rough still turns toward straw on banks.
- **No clean-up ring round the greens.** The green's own pattern now runs right to its edge, and the fringe marks where it ends. Tees keep theirs.

**Mowing styles.** Choose how fairways and greens are cut, in **Stripes**, a **Checkerboard** or **Diamonds**: **Fairway mowing** and **Green mowing** are in the course's turf settings. By default fairways are striped and greens are in diamonds, as before. Holes that share a fairway are mown as one. The ball plays the same whichever you pick.

**Course Builder.**
- **An Approach layer** for the mown ground in front of a green. It plays as fairway and is mown in finer diamonds, with a soft edge into the ground round it. It is yellow-lime on the plan.
- **The fringe** is teal on the plan and tinted in the review and the 3D editor, so it no longer looks the same as the fairway. In the game it is mown as one pass round its edge.
- **Fairway mowing**, **Approach mowing** and **Green mowing** are under Landscape in the Name step and in the 3D editor, and are saved with the course.
- Layers are picked from the **Layers** list again. The drop-down that appeared beside it is gone.

**In Settings.** *Graphics → The look of the course → Green colour lab…* lets you try other colours for a landscape's green and fringe while you look at the course, and copy what you chose to send to us.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.21, and a course file plays as it did. The ball's flight, the challenges, the course of the week and online play are as they were.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.21: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and builder drafts. A course with an Approach layer or its own mowing needs 0.9.22: an older copy leaves the approach out, playing whatever was painted under it, and mows the course its own way. **Everyone in an online room needs 0.9.22**, and a friend's challenge code is tied to the version it was made on, so make sure you both have 0.9.22.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
