# Fairway 0.9.4 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.4`, published 2026-10-23.

A speed update. Courses look and play as they did in 0.9.3; they just draw faster.

**Faster frames.** On the Ultra setting a frame takes about a fifth less time than before (measured on the redwood and Pacific Northwest courses), and the slowest frames -- the small hitches you might notice while aiming -- take about half as long. High is about a fifth faster too. Medium and Low gain less on a fast graphics card, but the work the game does on your computer's processor each frame is down by about a fifth on every setting, which is what helps most on older laptops and phones.

**What was made quicker, without changing the picture.**

- The screen around the course no longer redraws labels that have not changed, which was costing more than anything else.
- The small hole map keeps its course drawing and only redraws the ball, the aim line and the markers.
- Far-away shadows are refreshed a little less often (they still update the moment the view moves on), and on Ultra the sharpest shadow map near you is a little smaller, with its soft edges kept the same.
- Cloud shadows and morning mist skip their work wherever it cannot change a pixel.

We compared the old and new versions frame by frame, standing still and following a drive: the difference is a faint speckle on the nearest trees on Ultra and nothing you can see on the other settings.

**Nothing new to learn.** Every screen, setting and course is as it was, and your rounds, courses, profiles and medals carry over. Courses grow exactly as before.

**One thing to know.** A friend's challenge code is tied to the version it was made on, so a code made on 0.9.3 plays only on 0.9.3. If you are playing a friend, make sure you both have 0.9.4.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
