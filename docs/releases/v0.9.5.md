# Fairway 0.9.5 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.5`, published 2026-10-27.

A new look. Every course is redrawn in one hand-painted cartoon style, the landscapes are rebuilt around the holes, and there is a tenth landscape to play.

**A tenth landscape: Lakeshore Dunes.** A summer evening on a freshwater shore, after the dune courses of Lake Michigan: bright fairways between tall green dunes, beachgrass over the rough, cottonwood, oak and maple woods behind, and patches of raw blown sand that play as sand.

**Everything redrawn.** Every tree, shrub, cactus, palm, rock, house, flower, tee marker, bench and flag is new, made for Fairway in one style, along with the turf, the bunker sand, the water, the light and the shadows.

**Each landscape has its own country.** Between the holes the ground is what that place is made of: rock on the mountains and in the forests, hardpan and dry washes in the desert, heather and marram on the links, tall prairie grass in the Midwest, coral by the island's shore, pine straw and fallen leaves under the trees. **Each plays as what it is** -- a ball kicks high off rock and runs on, stops dead in heather, sits down in pine straw -- and the lie box names it. None of it is a hazard: you play it as it lies.

**Woods to the horizon.** The country beyond the course is real hills and forest out to the edge: the desert's mesas and buttes, the mountains' peaks, islets out to sea, Haunted Hollow's dead trees on the moor. Most courses are now cut out of forest that was never cleared, so a wild shot can end among the trees.

**Mown like a real course.** Greens are diamond-mowed, with a clean-up lap round the edge, and the diamonds swap light and dark as you walk round. Tees are striped with the line of play. Every flag carries its hole's number and flies downwind, harder the stronger the wind.

**Water that moves.** Deep water is darker, wavelets lap at the edges of ponds and lakes, surf rolls in on the sea, creeks ripple, and on High and Ultra the trees on the bank show in a calm pond.

**On the links,** two holes running side by side can share one wide fairway, as on the Old Course, with a pot bunker or two on the line between them.

**Reading the green.** The Clear way of showing the ground's slope is now the default, so the tilt of a green reads at a glance.

**A smaller download.** The game file is 11.4 MB, down from 17.7 MB.

**What it asks of your computer.** The new look asks more of the graphics card than 0.9.4 did, on every setting. On a modern card it still runs far faster than a golf game needs; if an older laptop struggles, try Low. North Sea Links is the heaviest course.

**Courses are new.** The same course code grows a different course than it did in 0.9.4, because every landscape was rebuilt. A round saved in 0.9.4 will tell you its course has changed. Your profiles, settings and medals carry over. A friend's challenge code is tied to the version it was made on, so make sure you both have 0.9.5.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
