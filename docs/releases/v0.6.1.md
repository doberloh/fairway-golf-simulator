# Fairway 0.6.1 (beta)

Tag `v0.6.1`, published 2026-10-11.

A rework of what you see while you play: your shot numbers, launch monitor play, aiming and the cameras.

**Your shot numbers, live and anywhere you want them.** Every number you pick in *Shot data* is now a small tile of its own on the course. Drag each one wherever you like; it stays there. The moment you hit, the launch numbers appear, and carry and total count up as the ball flies — carry stops where it lands, total keeps going through the roll. A slim bar along the bottom carries the shot's status, Skip and the countdown, and its arrow hides or shows all the tiles. *Reset layout* in Tools puts everything back. **Hang time** now shows the time the ball is in the air; it used to show the time until it stopped rolling.

**A launch monitor window.** With your monitor armed, the shot controls step aside and a small window takes their place: the ball light (flashing red with no monitor, amber while it looks for a ball, green when it is ready), the Armed switch, your club and Reconnect. Turn Armed off and the normal controls come back. Aim with the mouse, the map or the keyboard: **↑ ↓ move your aim point further or shorter** for every club, the putter included, and a putt starts aimed at the hole. Playing by hand and playing with a monitor each keep their own choice of numbers and their own tile positions.

**Plays like.** Under the yards to the hole you now see what the shot plays like — "333, plays 322" — allowing for the wind, the hole being above or below you, altitude and temperature. It's worked out by Fairway's own ball flight for the club in your hands, so it matches how the ball will actually fly.

**Shots filmed like television.** The camera you aimed from stays put and turns to follow the ball, so you see its height and its curve. Once it's well past its highest point the view cuts to a camera waiting beside where it lands, which then swings round behind the ball as it runs out. Chips are followed from behind. When the ball stops, the camera rises to show the whole shot from the side, then flies on to your next one — Enter skips it, and *Camera settings* can turn it off. The fly-down onto each new hole is slower and passes through the trees instead of hopping over them.

**New keys.** **A** puts your aim back, **I/K** change club (as well as Q/E), **G** flies to your aim point, then the green, then back, **H** hides everything over the course, and **Tab** lists every key.

**Smaller fixes.** The aim point and yards-to-hole labels no longer sit on top of each other — they take turns while you aim. The scorecard's hole row says *Hole*. *Launch* is now *Launch angle*. The course creator no longer draws a shot line off the first tee.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code, and `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Your players, saved rounds and courses from 0.6 carry over, and every course grows exactly the same ground. Your chosen shot numbers carry over too; their old column setting does not, since the numbers are tiles now. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting.
