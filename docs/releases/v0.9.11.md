# Fairway 0.9.11 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.11`, published 2026-11-09.

Large courses run much faster on every graphics setting, and the Course Builder opens them sooner.

**Faster on big courses.** Courses made in the Course Builder from real land can be huge: Dinosaur Mountain has more than 45,000 trees and 600 houses. On the PC it was measured on, its frames now take a fraction of the time they did: about a fifth on Low and under half on Medium, High and Ultra. The generated courses run at least as fast as before.

**Trees far away.** On every setting a distant tree is drawn as a simpler version of itself, and it turns into that version with a short dissolve instead of switching at a line. Each simpler version keeps its tree's outline; the few plants whose shape would not survive it (among them the ocotillo, the hala and the sotol) are always drawn in full.

**Low draws fewer distant trees.** Far from where you are playing, Low thins the trees out gradually, and they fade back in as you come closer. It never thins the hole you are on or the trees around your ball, and in free flight it keeps every tree near the camera. Every tree is still there for the ball to hit on every setting.

**Distant ground.** On the very largest courses the far ground is drawn more simply, never more than a pixel or two from the ground the ball meets.

**Course Builder.** Game view opens a large course in about 30 seconds instead of 45 to 50. It now shows what the picture costs (frames a second, and how much is being drawn) while you build, and a course with more than 25,000 trees or 400 houses gets a note saying how many of its trees stand far from play.

**Your saves.** Courses, saved rounds, settings and friend challenge codes all carry over from 0.9.10 unchanged.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
