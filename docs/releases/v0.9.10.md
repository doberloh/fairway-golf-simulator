# Fairway 0.9.10 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.10`, published 2026-11-09.

New trees and plants for many of the landscapes, and rockier desert ground for courses built from real land.

**Remade trees and plants.** Twenty-two plants were remade from photographs of the real thing, and each comes in two shapes:

- **Redwood Coast:** bay laurel, madrone, bigleaf maple and a young redwood sapling.
- **Hawaiian Resort:** plumeria and bougainvillea, their flowers in their own colours.
- **The deserts:** ocotillo, creosote, brittlebush, teddy-bear cholla and Desert Resort's African sumac.
- **Florida Palms and Lowcountry:** muhly grass, its pink plumes standing apart on their own stalks.
- **Badlands:** sagebrush.
- **Every landscape with round stones:** a new plain boulder.

**Lighter coast greens.** The coast live oak, shore pine and huckleberry are a few shades lighter, so they no longer read as near black.

**Fewer buildings.** The barn and silo are gone from Southern Pines and Lowcountry and Gulf, and the ruin from Desert Mountain and Desert Resort.

**Desert Mountain from real land.** A course made in the Course Builder from real land on Desert Mountain or Desert Resort now has the desert's hard floor around its rock, not sand. Where one map boundary holds two courses that both number 1 to 18, like Troon North's Monument and Pinnacle, the Course Builder now lists them as two courses.

**Your saves.** Courses, saved rounds and settings carry over from 0.9.9. A round saved on Southern Pines, Lowcountry and Gulf, Desert Mountain or Desert Resort shows a notice when you open it, because the ground there has changed slightly. Friend challenge codes from 0.9.9 do not play on 0.9.10.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
