# Fairway 0.2 (beta)

Tag `v0.2`, published 2026-10-06.

The second beta of Fairway.

**Fixed: shots went the wrong way sideways.** A shot your launch monitor reported starting or curving left flew right on screen, and the other way round, while the shot readout still agreed with the monitor. The same was true of shot shapes set with the keyboard or controller. Launch direction and spin axis now mean what your monitor means: positive is right. If you play with a monitor, please update.

**New**

- Every panel on the play screen (shot controls, map, weather, the tools strip, the course card and the flight controls) drags by a title bar like the tool windows have. On a phone a small corner grip does the same job.
- Simulator bay: enter your screen's width and height in inches instead of a diagonal and a shape. The shape is worked out for you, and the view is drawn in your screen's shape however the game's window is resized. A bay saved in 0.1 is converted automatically. The mat's left/right setting now moves in tenths of a foot.
- The driving range's Range controls can be reopened from the Tools window after you close them.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code, and `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.

This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting.
