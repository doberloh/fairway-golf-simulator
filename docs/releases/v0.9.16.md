# Fairway 0.9.16 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.16`, published 2026-11-15.

Fairway runs much better on a phone, and green reading is easier to see on every screen.

**Phones get their own graphics setting.** An iPhone or an Android phone now starts on a new **Phone** setting every time the game opens, with the frame rate capped at 30. On our test iPhone the game used to run at about 14 to 20 frames a second on a full course; it now holds a steady 30. To get there the phone draws:

- every tree on the hole you are playing and around your ball, and far fewer away from it;
- shadows from simpler copies of the trees, redrawn a few times a second;
- the ground painted into pictures around the camera now and then, then drawn from them with live light and shadow;
- half as much grass around you, and simpler trees a little sooner in the distance.

Settings, Graphics still offers the other settings on a phone for that session, and **Paint the ground once** turns the painted ground off if you want to compare. On a PC, Low to Ultra are exactly as they were.

**Why a cap of 30.** A phone's graphics chip runs at full speed only for a short burst, then settles at well under half of it. A steady 30 looks smoother than a frame rate that jumps around, and it keeps the phone cooler.

**Green reading, on from the start.** The slope grid and the downhill markers are now on by default on every device (the heat map stays off). The grid's lines are drawn about a point and a half wide on any screen, so they show on a phone too. The markers' speed now tells you how much break there is: a slow crawl on gentle slopes, quick on steep ones.

**Measure and watch your device.** Under Help, *Report a problem*, two new buttons time the game on your own device. **Measure this device** takes the picture apart piece by piece (about a minute and a half). **Watch this device** records how your last few minutes of play ran and times the device itself (about two and a half minutes). Both add what they find to *Copy diagnostic*, which you can paste to us. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.15. The ball, the scoring, the challenges and the course of the week are as they were.

**Known.** On a phone you may notice a brief pause now and then as the ground is repainted, and about half a second when a new hole first appears. No Android phone has been tried yet.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.15: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and friend challenge codes.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
