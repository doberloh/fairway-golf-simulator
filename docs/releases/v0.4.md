# Fairway 0.4 (beta)

Tag `v0.4`, published 2026-10-07.

The fourth beta of Fairway.

**New: water and out of bounds follow the Rules of Golf.** A ball in the water no longer sends you straight back to where you hit. It stops, and a bar at the foot of the screen offers what the Rules allow, each for one penalty stroke: play again from where you hit, drop back on the line through where the ball went in, or drop within two club-lengths of where it went in, no nearer the hole. Out of bounds is one stroke and play again. In **Format & tees** you can switch out of bounds to **Local Rule E-5**, the casual-play rule many clubs use: drop near where the ball went out, toward the fairway, for two strokes. It works in stroke play, match play, scramble and endless runs, and survives a reload or a mulligan. Choose with a click, a tap or the arrow keys.

**New: back up your players.** **My profile → Save a backup file** keeps every player, their stats and their history; **Restore from a backup** brings them back on another browser, computer or phone, and adds to the players already there rather than replacing them. The first-time welcome offers the restore too.

**New: remove a round from your history.** A practice round you would rather not count can be taken out, so its scores leave your scoring and your sim handicap.

**A cleaner look.** The whole game now uses one typeface, the one the logo has always been drawn in, and the hole, par, yards and pin at the top line up properly at every screen size. The browser tab reads just "Fairway", and the main menu shows the logo on its own.

**Fixed**

- Building a course showed a full progress bar that sat still for most of the wait while the graphics were prepared. The bar now keeps moving the whole way.
- Credits for two small pieces of open-source shader code were missing from the licences; they are now in **Help → Open source & credits**.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code, and `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Your players and saved rounds from 0.3 carry over. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting.
