# Fairway 0.8.3 (beta)

Tag `v0.8.3`, published 2026-10-16.

A better driving range: real target greens, a short-game green, and your session to take home.

**The targets are real greens.** Every target on the range is now a mown green with a fringe, a cup and a flag, so a ball that lands on one checks and rolls the way it would on a course, and can go in. Each number is now the true distance from the centre mat to the middle of that green -- the 50 really is 50 yards away.

**Aim at a green to play to it.** From the mats, the green you aim at becomes the one you are playing to: its cup is live and the yardage on the card is to it.

**A chipping and putting green.** Beside the mats on your right. In **Range controls**, *Hit from* sets you up to **Chip** from 5 to 60 yards or **Putt** from 3 to 80 feet, on it or on the centre green, with *Play it out until it drops* and *A new spot every ball*.

**Shape the greens.** The centre green and the short-game green can each be flat, slope left to right, slope right to left, or have random contours like a course's greens -- with **New green** and **New cup**. The green-reading tools work on a sloped range green.

**Take your session home.** *Export CSV* saves every shot for a spreadsheet. *PDF report* saves a printable report: each club's averages and spread, a chart of where your shots went, your short game, and every shot.

**Range controls is always one click away**, from its own button on the toolbar down the right-hand side.

**Trees on Low and Medium graphics** no longer break up into floating shards or striped crowns.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

Everything from 0.8.2 carries over: your rounds, courses, profiles and medals. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or device.
