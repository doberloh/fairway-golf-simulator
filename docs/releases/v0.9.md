# Fairway 0.9 (beta)

Tag `v0.9`, published 2026-10-18.

A whole new look: the interface that Fairway 1.0 will ship with, on every screen.

**A new interface, everywhere.** Leaning panels, bold condensed lettering and colours that change with the landscape, so a round in the desert looks like the desert and the menus wear this week's course. It covers every screen: the round, the main menu, Play, the course of the week, challenges, Endless, saved courses, My profile and the locker, settings, the launch monitor, the driving range, the course studio, pause, the scorecard and the big map. On a phone, every screen is laid out for the phone.

**The round screen.** Everything sits in the corners: the hole top left, the wind at the top, a card for every golfer top right, the course map bottom right and your club bottom left, with the Swing Meter (or, with a launch monitor armed, the ball light) along the foot. The screen clears while the ball is in the air.

**A shot card over every shot.** When the ball stops and the camera pulls back, a card comes up above the top of the shot's line with an arrow down to it: the total and the yards left to the pin in big figures, then ball speed, backspin and apex. **Tab** shows every number. The map and the Tab numbers can be moved.

**A title-screen main menu.** One list down the left and your golfer's card on the right, driven with the arrow keys or a controller's d-pad. Fairway now opens on a start screen in the course of the week's colours, with a tip while it gets ready.

**Play, step by step.** Choose the course, your golfers, the format and the rules, one step at a time, with a little 3D model of each course on its card and a picture of every course drawn from above. Each golfer plays from their own tees. The course of the week, Endless and the driving range are set up the same way.

**New sounds.** The interface has a new set of clicks and chimes, and finishing a hole plays a short piece of chiptune for your score.

**New icons.** The home-screen and tab icons wear the new colours.

**Smaller things.** Text sizes up properly from the start of a round: automatic resolution now only judges frames while you play, so the menus no longer start a round blurry. Seat pickers in the golfer menus line up with their seats, and the info buttons sit beside the names they explain.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

Everything from 0.8.3 carries over: your rounds, courses, profiles and medals, and courses grow exactly as before. This is a beta: the new interface is new, it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or device, and if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
