# Fairway 0.9.2 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.2`, published 2026-10-22.

A big tidy of the whole interface: every menu, setup screen, the round screen and phones, gone over seven times and fixed as we went.

**One Play for every way to play.** Play opens on what you want to play: the **course of the week**, **Endless**, a **saved course**, a **friend's challenge**, **Surprise me** or a **new course in the studio**. Each has its own step, then the same Golfers, Format and Rules for all of them. On a first start, Tee off goes straight to the course of the week.

**Challenges have their own place on the main menu.** Pick a challenge, choose who is playing, and play by rules that are the same for everyone. The results now look like Round complete: the challenge in large letters, a row for each golfer with their medal and score.

**Esc goes back a step.** On Play, Challenges and the driving range, Esc (or the Back button at the top) goes back one step at a time, so a mistake no longer throws away the golfers and format you picked. From the first step it closes the screen.

**Leaving a round, and coming back.** Pick Main menu while the ball is still flying and it lands first, then you're asked whether to save. After **Save and leave**, the main menu offers **Continue** to pick the round straight back up. Your saved rounds, and **Import a round from a file**, are always at the foot of Play's first step.

**The round screen.** The shot numbers sit on the left. **?** lists every key over the course. Esc pauses with six rows: Resume, Scorecard, Golfers & rules, Settings, Help & controls and Main menu. Settings in a round is one window with a tab for each kind of setting, and **T** opens Tools. Your first swing is coached: a small box spells out the three taps until you have swung once.

**Round complete.** The result comes first, by what the format counts: who won, a tie called a tie, skins won, or the match result. Then the card, then each golfer's row with their XP, level and handicap. The buttons stay in reach at the bottom of the screen even with a long card.

**A clearer view.** The play camera no longer looks through a tree, a rock or a house standing in front of it. Free flight keeps them out of the way until you move. Windows such as the range controls open beside the action, not over the shot numbers or the tools. The small map stays whole after you open the big map.

**On a phone.** The picture is moved up so the swing meter, the lie and the shot shape never cover the ball or the aim line, held either way up. Help topics and the profile's tabs come into view when you pick them, the setup steps and settings fit an upright screen, and nothing runs off the edge.

**Launch monitors.** The launch monitor screen lists every monitor that rēlā connects to Fairway, in one box: Foresight GC2, SkyTrak, FlightScope Mevo+ and Mevo Gen2, FlightScope Xi and X2, Garmin R10, Full Swing KIT, Square, Nova and GolfJoy. None of them has been tried with Fairway yet.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

Everything from 0.9.1 carries over: your rounds, courses, profiles and medals, and courses grow exactly as before. A friend's challenge code is tied to the version it was made on, so a code made on 0.9.1 plays only on 0.9.1. Send new ones from 0.9.2. This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
