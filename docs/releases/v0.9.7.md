# Fairway 0.9.7 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.7`, published 2026-11-05.

The whole of the USA to play, a way to build real courses, and new trees on the Redwood Coast.

**Forty-two landscapes.** Course studio now covers every region of the USA, from Alaska to the Keys, plus Scottish Links, each with its own ground, rock, plants and light. Choose one from a map of the country. Each landscape's terrain is researched from its real geology, and the land rolls on from hole to hole, with no hole sitting in a bowl of its own.

**Fairway Course Builder (beta).** *Fairway Builder.html*, beside the game in every download, builds a course from a real place: trace it from a map or from LIDAR height data, shape its land and greens, plant its trees, and play it in the game. It is new and it is large, so expect rough edges.

**Seasons you can see.** Winter bares the broadleaves, snow lies on the trees in a snowy winter, and spring brings bloom to the landscapes that have it.

**New trees on the Redwood Coast.** Redwoods, firs, hemlocks, red cedars and tanoaks are all new, and the landscape draws noticeably faster on the Low setting. Some of these trees also grow in other landscapes. The darkest trees no longer turn nearly black.

**Paths and bridges on every course.** Cart paths have rounded corners and loop back rather than stopping dead, and every path over water is a bridge.

**What has gone.** Architect courses, Haunted Hollow, tournament week and the people on the course are removed. Architect's best parts, its paths, bridges, walls and landmarks, are now on every course.

**Your saved games.** A round saved in 0.9.6 is told its course has changed, and a saved Architect or Haunted Hollow course shows a notice. Settings carry over. Friend challenge codes from 0.9.6 do not play on 0.9.7.

**A bigger download.** The game file is 41.2 MB, up from 14.8 MB, for the landscapes, their models and pictures, and the Course Builder adds 31.1 MB of its own. A slower connection or an older phone will feel the first load.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta, and Architect courses are a beta inside it: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
