# Fairway 0.9.20 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.20`, published 2026-11-21.

Play a live round with friends in other houses. Online play is new and marked **BETA** in the game.

**Play online (beta).** On the main menu. One of you chooses *Start a room* and reads the room code out, or texts it; the others choose *Join a room* and type it in. The code is the only thing anyone types: no accounts, no sign-up. You let each friend in by name, choose the course and format as usual, and tee off. The round is on every screen at once: each of you swings on your own turn, with the swing pad or your launch monitor, and watches everyone else's ball fly and land in the same place. Your golfer, bag, stats and XP stay on your own device and count your own shots.

**Any mix of copies.** The download opened from disk, the game on the website, and a phone that opened the game from **run_fairway_server** on your PC, with your launch monitor on that PC, can all play in one room.

**Dropping in and out.** A room holds eight screens: four golfers, and anyone else watching. A friend who turns up mid-round can be let in to watch or to play; whoever the host seats joins the group at the next tee, never halfway through a hole, and their card starts there. Someone whose connection drops comes back to their own seat; someone who leaves and knocks again gets their own golfer back, card and all. If a friend's connection is gone for good, the host can play on without them after 20 seconds.

**When the connection wobbles.** A dropped connection comes back by itself, and one that has quietly died (a phone moving off Wi-Fi, a laptop that slept) is noticed and remade within about half a minute. A shot or choice made while you are reconnecting is turned back at once, to make again. If the host never comes back, or your screen cannot reach the room, after two minutes you can leave the round or keep waiting. If Fairway's online service cannot be reached at all, the game says so and keeps trying.

**Between holes** the next tee waits until everyone has seen the scorecard. The room code is shown only in the pause menu during a round (*Online room*), not on the screen. If the host leaves mid-round, everyone else is told so and taken back to the menu, without being asked to save a round that was the host's. A computer golfer's shots are shown on your screen the way your own settings say (every shot, near the green, or results only).

**Private.** The code is the room's key. Messages pass through Fairway's online service sealed with it, so the service cannot read them, and it keeps nothing. Nobody plays without the host's say. Keep a code among the group, and start a new room if it gets out. Fairway connects to the internet only while you are in a room.

**Also.** A computer golfer added in Play stays a computer golfer when you go back and change the game type; before, it could turn into an ordinary new player with its name.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.19. The ball's flight, the challenges and the course of the week are as they were. Played alone, the game plays exactly as before.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.19: courses, saved rounds, settings and profiles. **Everyone in a room needs 0.9.20**, and a friend's challenge code is tied to the version it was made on, so make sure you both have 0.9.20.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
