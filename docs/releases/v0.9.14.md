# Fairway 0.9.14 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.14`, published 2026-11-12.

The course of the week is a proper, full-length course now, and so is the Challenge Course.

**The course of the week was far too short.** Every week's course had been coming out at about 4,700 yards and par 68, with every par 4 at 300 yards and every par 3 at 120. That was a mistake in how the week's course was asked for, not a choice. It is now a full eighteen: between 6,400 and 6,850 yards from the blue tees (about 5,750 to 6,230 from the white), par 71 or 72, and a different length each week. It is still the same course for everyone on this version.

**A better course of the week.** Its settings changed too. Fewer holes bend, so more of them play straight from the tee. Fairways are a little wider with less semi-rough. Greens are a little larger with more shape to their edges, and few are raised, sunken or false-fronted. Bunkers have more shape and fewer sit out in the fairway. There is less water in play, apart from one lake, and there are more trees.

**This week's course changed with it.** The change starts with the week of Monday 9 November, so the course you may already have played this week is replaced by the full-length one. A best you saved on the short course earlier this week still shows; from Monday everyone starts level. Earlier weeks and their scores are left as they were.

**Surprise me.** A surprise nine is grown on the same settings: the same fairways, greens, water and trees, with a new landscape, length, lie of the land and wind each time.

**A new Challenge Course.** The course the challenges are played on had the same mistake and was 4,650 yards long. It is now a full-length course on the same settings: par 71, 6,480 yards from the blue tees. Every challenge has a new hole and new spots for its balls. Your medals and bests are kept, but they were won on the old course, so expect the scores to beat to feel different. The challenge of the week is played on the course of the week, as before.

**Course Builder.** *Generate and modify a course* opens on the same settings, and every slider can still be moved. A course file you open there keeps its own settings.

**Not changed.** Endless still rolls its holes as widely as ever. The driving range, the game's own Course studio, your saved courses, saved rounds and course codes are exactly as they were.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.13: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and friend challenge codes.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
