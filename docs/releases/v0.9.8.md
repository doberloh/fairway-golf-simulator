# Fairway 0.9.8 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.8`, published 2026-11-08.

Fifteen landscapes that look like American golf courses, with new trees in every one.

**Fifteen landscapes, not forty-two.** Classic Parkland, Southern Pines, Lowcountry and Gulf, Florida Palms, Hawaiian Resort, Desert Mountain, Desert Resort, Seaside Links, California Coast, Pacific Northwest, Redwood Coast, Mountain, Prairie & Sand Hills, Badlands and Northern Woods. The forty-two of 0.9.7 were an ecology catalogue; these are the looks real courses have, more manicured and with fewer kinds of tree. Each is chosen from the map of the country, which now has all fifteen on it.

**New trees everywhere.** Every landscape's trees are new, taller and slimmer than before, drawn from photographs of each tree on real courses.

**Courses that sit in their country.** Desert Mountain's holes run along the foot of its mountains, with the rock beside the fairways, and about one or two ponds in eighteen holes. The Badlands' holes wind between their banded clay formations.

**No clubhouse, cart paths or road for now.** A generated course has no clubhouse, cart paths, public road or parking lot in this version. They will be back once they sit better in the new landscapes. A course built in the Course Builder keeps its own paths.

**Maps with shape.** The course map and the hole map are shaded, lit from the top left, so hills and hollows read at a glance.

**Night golf on long courses.** On a long eighteen, including courses from the Course Builder, the ground and the trees could disappear at night with the floodlights on. Fixed: as many floodlights as your graphics card can take are lit, the nearest to you first, up to 96.

**Smaller fixes.** Bare trees in winter no longer break into stray branches, and the bougainvillea no longer glows neon.

**Your saved games.** A course saved on a landscape that has gone opens on the landscape that took its place, for example the Rockies on Mountain or the Hill Country on Prairie & Sand Hills. A round saved in 0.9.7 is told its course has changed. Settings carry over. Friend challenge codes from 0.9.7 do not play on 0.9.8.

**A bigger download.** The game file is 57.3 MB, up from 41.2 MB, almost all of it the new trees. The Course Builder is 52.9 MB. A slower connection or an older phone will feel the first load.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
