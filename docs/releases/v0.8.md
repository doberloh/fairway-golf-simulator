# Fairway 0.8 (beta)

Tag `v0.8`, published 2026-10-15.

The Swing Meter, XP and levels, things to unlock and wear, challenges and a course of the week -- and greens at their real size.

**The Swing Meter.** Playing with the keyboard, the mouse, a touch screen or a controller, you now swing with a three-tap meter at the foot of the course, the way Hot Shots Golf and PGA 2K do it: tap to start, tap to set the power, tap in the strike zone. **Space** taps it, so does **A / Cross** on a controller, or a tap anywhere once a swing has started. A blue marker shows where to stop for the ball to land in your aim circle. Before you swing, choose the **curve** (S: draw, straight or fade), the **height** (W: low, normal or high -- a bump and run or a flop near the green) and the **spin** (X: every step you add narrows the strike zone). Putts are two taps. The meter keeps carry, total and apex on screen after the shot. Each golfer sets their own difficulty -- Easy, Normal or Hard -- so a group can play on different settings. It replaces the power slider, the shot-shape sliders and the ball-flight profile.

**The lie costs ball speed.** Semi-rough keeps 96% of it, rough 90% and sand 80%, and the shot controls say so before you swing. Launch-monitor shots lose the same, unless you switch it off for the round in *Format & tees*. Launch-monitor golfers and Swing Meter golfers can play in the same group.

**XP and levels.** Every golfer earns XP for how they play -- finishing a hole, the score on it, fairways, greens, a shot that lands on the number, chip-ins and long putts, a first round of the day. A small popup shows what you earned and your level bar filling. Levels never stop; up to level 50, every level brings something. Existing profiles start at level 1, with nothing taken away.

**Things to unlock and wear.** On **My profile → Your look**: ball finishes (eight plain colours from the start, then from Red stripe to Galaxy, Lava, Gold and Rainbow), tracer styles (plain colours, then Sky, Comet, Smoke, Fire, Lightning, Aurora, Rainbow and more, each shown moving), frames round your picture (animated ones that burn, sparkle, ripple, crackle with lightning or drift with leaves, and leave the circle), titles, badges, meter skins, and flourishes that play over the cup when you hole out -- par first, then birdie, eagle, chip-in. Locked things show in full colour with a padlock and the level that brings them. Your tracer is your own in every mode.

**Course of the week, challenges and achievements.** A new 18-hole course every Monday, the same on every copy of Fairway, with no account: stroke play, your best this week and your best ever. **Challenges**, all open from the start: closest to the pin, long drive, up and down, and a skins game. Thirteen **achievements**, some bringing a title to wear.

**Greens at their real size.** Greens are about half the size they were, close to a typical course (around 6,000 square feet), with the same slopes to read. A new **Green size** setting in Course studio makes them anywhere from 60% to 160%. The flagstick is taller and thicker so you can see it -- eight feet, two inches across at the top -- and when you putt it stays in the hole while you line up, then lifts out as the ball comes near.

**Smaller things.** Whose turn it is flashes up at the start of each turn, and the top-bar circle shows your ball, frame and level. The camera stays with a rolling putt, sits low behind the ball when you aim one, and flies straight through trees on its way up to show the tracer and back. The ball rattles in the cup properly. Course studio's settings fill the screen. A new build no longer freezes for a couple of seconds the first time you start a round from the main menu, and the camera's pull-back after you hole out is smoother.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code, and `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Your players, saved courses and course codes from 0.7 carry over. Because greens changed size, a round saved in 0.7 opens with a note that its course has been rebuilt: carry on where you are, or start that course afresh. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting.
