# Fairway 0.6 (beta)

Tag `v0.6`, published 2026-10-10.

A new landscape for Halloween, and a smoother game, especially at the higher graphics settings.

**A ninth landscape: Haunted Hollow.** A course where nothing is alive, and it is full fantasy: purple fairways and violet rough leading to slime-green greens, under gnarled dead oaks, tall pale snags and bare willows, with the heaviest fog of any landscape and a low orange sun under a mauve sky. Giant pumpkins grow in patches in the rough, some taller than you, with toadstools the size of a person round the trunks -- and they are solid, like the boulders, so play around them. Most of the pumpkins are carved, and their faces light up and flicker as the sun goes down. Ghosts drift on their rounds over every hole; a ball flies straight through them. As the light goes, a huge orange harvest moon rises in the east, bats wheel round the tallest dead trees, and will-o'-the-wisps drift over the ponds and settle in the hollows. It is at its best from Golden hour on. Choose it in Course studio, and Surprise me can grow one too.

**The time-of-day slider is smooth again.** Dragging the clock used to stutter every few steps, and on a course with a lot of water it could stall for a moment each time the light changed. Now the day runs from dawn to dusk under your finger at full speed, at every graphics setting.

**No more freeze when you turn toward the sun.** On High and Ultra, the first time you looked toward the sun after a course appeared -- often while flying around in Course studio -- the game could lock up for several seconds while it prepared the light shafts. That work now happens behind the loading screen.

**Faster with the sun in front of you.** On High and Ultra, the light shafts were quietly drawing every shadow in the scene a second time. They don't any more, so frames looking into the sun are noticeably lighter on the graphics card.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code, and `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.

Your players, saved rounds and courses from 0.5.1 carry over, and every course grows exactly the same ground. This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting.
