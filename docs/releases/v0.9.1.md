# Fairway 0.9.1 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.1`, published 2026-10-20.

Challenge a friend to beat your card, and a round of fixes from playing 0.9.

**Challenge a friend.** Finish a stroke-play round of 9 or 18 holes and press **Challenge a friend** under Round complete. It copies a code holding the course, your score on every hole and every shot you hit -- paste it into a message, or save it as a file for an app that will not take one that long. Your friend pastes it on **Play → A friend's challenge** (or into Saved courses' course code box) and plays the same course against you: your shots are drawn on each hole as white dashed lines, your card sits on their scorecard, and the line above it says who is ahead. Lowest total wins, and every hole's winner is shown. You both need the same version of Fairway.

**Round complete, tidied up.** The scorecard itself is now at the top. Under it, the standings carry each golfer's XP, level and handicap on their own row, and the buttons are one row: Main menu, New round, Save card and Challenge a friend.

**A Next hole button.** Close the scorecard after holing out to look round the hole's shot lines, and a **Next hole** button (or Enter) waits at the bottom of the screen. After the last hole it takes you to the final scorecard.

**Turn the camera round.** In the aim point and green views (**G**), drag on the course to turn the camera round the spot it is looking at, and up or down; the mouse wheel moves it in and out. A click without a drag still aims.

**Which camera you are on.** A small box beside the wind names the view: behind the ball, aim point, green, overview, free flight or flyover.

**The aim point says more, for longer.** Its distance now stays on screen for three seconds after you stop aiming, and it also tells you how far that spot leaves you from the hole.

**Saving a course in the studio** asks for its name in a small box over the landscape: **Save**, or **Go back** to carry on shaping.

**Saved courses.** Play, Rename, Copy code and Delete are small buttons on each course's card. Delete asks for a second press, so one stray click cannot lose a course.

**Course cards are one piece.** On Play, Endless and Saved courses, a course's picture and the box under it now form one slanted card, so it is clear they are one button. The odd shapes some pictures had are gone.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

Everything from 0.9 carries over: your rounds, courses, profiles and medals, and courses grow exactly as before. This is a beta: these changes have not been tried on every device, and if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
