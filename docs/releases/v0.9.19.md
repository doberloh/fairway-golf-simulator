# Fairway 0.9.19 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.19`, published 2026-11-19.

Play against computer golfers, follow a whole season on the Fairway Tour, and score your rounds net or in Stableford. The computer golfers and everything built on them are new and marked **BETA** in the game.

**Computer golfers (beta).** Any seat in your group can be a computer golfer: pick *Computer golfer* in a seat's name list, or press *Add computer golfer*. Each plays like a golfer of their handicap, misses included, from a tour pro to a weekend hacker. *Matched* plays to your level, and *Adaptive* gets better or worse with you through the round. In the rules you choose how much of their play you watch, every shot or just near the green, and whether they say a word after a good or bad one. Their aim line is not shown, so you don't see where they are about to miss.

**The Fairway Tour (beta).** On the main menu: a season of tournaments against a field of computer golfers, with a leaderboard, a cut after two rounds, majors and a season on points. Play a Quick season or set one up on your own courses, and share a tour with friends as one file, built courses and all.

**Handicaps and Stableford.** Play any round *Gross* or *Net*: each golfer's handicap strokes come off on the holes they get them. **Stableford** joins the formats: two points for a par, three for a birdie, one for a bogey, so one bad hole costs you no more than zero.

**The caddie and strokes gained (beta).** The caddie suggests a club before your shot (on the swing pad by default). At the end of a round, and on My profile, **strokes gained** shows where you won and lost shots against a scratch golfer: off the tee, approach, around the green and putting.

**Order of play.** In the rules: *Hole out in turn*, as before, or *Rules of golf*: the honour tees off first, then the ball farthest from the hole plays next, whoever's it is. With computer golfers you play alongside them rather than waiting for each to finish the hole. Match play's honour now goes to the side that won the last hole.

**The camera follows the flight again.** It trails your ball on the line it was struck on, and no longer circles round the ball near the pin. A putt is followed from behind, and the camera stops two metres short of the cup to watch the ball in. The television camera is still there: *Shot camera* in Camera settings.

**Easier to see.** The gimme circle and the putting rings are bold, and the ring your rolling ball is inside turns pink. The aim line is blaze orange, so it shows on the palest greens.

**Also.** A water drop is never placed on ground too steep for the ball to stay on: on one coast hole it could land you on a cliff above the sea. A computer golfer that reaches five over par on a hole picks up and drops by the green, so a hole always ends. The rules on Play now show every setting: some new ones were hidden there before. The map no longer redraws its whole background at every tee shot.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.18. The ball's flight, the challenges and the course of the week are as they were. With a launch monitor the game plays exactly as before.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.18: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and friend challenge codes. A round saved before this plays in the old order, each golfer holing out in turn.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
