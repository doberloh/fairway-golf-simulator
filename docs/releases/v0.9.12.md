# Fairway 0.9.12 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.12`, published 2026-11-10.

The Course Builder, made easy: find a real course by name, box it on the map, and build it for you or by hand. Built courses can now have up to six sets of tees.

**Find it, box it, build it.** One search bar finds a course by name and shows the map around it. Drag a box round the course, then choose **Build it for me** (a reviewed course in one press, with a warning that it may not always get the map right) or **Build it myself**. The land starts building as soon as you move on; there is no extra button to press.

**Checking the map.** The builder reads OpenStreetMap's holes against the course and points out holes that look wrong: one drawn to the wrong green, two sharing a green, lines crossing the rough. It tells you what needs fixing by hand instead of guessing.

**Drawing like a paint program.** Door 2 has menus, a step bar, a tool palette and a status bar. The pen closes and fills its shape when you click its first point again or press Enter. The brush is steadied. Layers show which one you are painting on, and where a stroke went under a higher one. There is Measure with live yardages, copy, paste, duplicate and delete, a named undo with a history, a hole navigator and an overview map, a sharper aerial picture with its brightness, contrast and date, and Skin from the land. Your work is kept as you go and offered back if the page closes.

**The 3D editor.** The same brush and pen, its tools as an icon palette, and holes can be added and removed.

**Six tee sets.** Built courses can have up to six sets of tees: black, blue, white, silver, gold and red, longest first. A course built from OpenStreetMap takes its sets from the map. Each golfer's tee buttons, Playing tee and the scorecard show the course's own sets, and a golfer on a set the course does not have plays the nearest one. Generated courses keep blue, white and red. On built courses the tee markers stay inside narrow tee boxes.

**The builder's guide.** Every download carries **Fairway Builder Guide.html**, a guide to the Course Builder with pictures, that opens without an internet connection. First-visit tips explain each screen once.

**Your saves.** Courses, saved rounds, settings and friend challenge codes all carry over from 0.9.11 unchanged, and course files built before play exactly as they did.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
