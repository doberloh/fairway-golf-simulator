# Fairway 0.9.17 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.17`, published 2026-11-15.

The woods around a course no longer stop in a straight line at the edge of the property.

**The woods carry on past the boundary.** On a wooded course, the trees used to end in a ruled wall at the property line, with open meadow straight beyond it, and the trees just outside the line stood much thinner than the ones just inside. Now the woods along each side of the course carry on past the line at the same thickness, then thin out raggedly into the surrounding country over the next couple of hundred metres. Where a hole runs close to the boundary and the course is open there, the land beyond stays open too. You will notice it most from the air and from the tee boxes near the edge on the Redwood Coast, Pacific Northwest, Northern Woods, Classic Parkland, Southern Pines and the Gulf Coast. Courses beside the sea, the Badlands and Desert Mountain look as they did.

**Courses you built.** The same goes for a course from the Course Builder: its own trees carry on past its line, then give way to the surroundings you chose. If you set Trees to *No trees*, there are still none at all.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.16: the trees past the line are scenery, never anything your ball can hit. The frame rate is the same on every graphics setting (measured on Ultra and Low). The ball, the scoring, the challenges and the course of the week are as they were.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.16: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and friend challenge codes.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
