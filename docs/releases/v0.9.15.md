# Fairway 0.9.15 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.15`, published 2026-11-14.

The land around every course has been rebuilt, the Course Builder can choose it, and the game draws its trees much faster on High and Ultra.

**The country past the course.** The land nobody plays on, from the property line to the horizon, used to be a coarse ring with "tree domes" and a tree pattern painted on the hills. It is real country now. The ground is about fourteen times finer, so ridges and slopes have shape. The trees out there are the landscape's own trees, at their true size, as far as you can see, with open meadow among the woods. They are lit like everything else at every hour, and they change with the season.

**Mountains.** On Mountain, Desert Resort and Hawaiian Resort the far mountainsides were reworked: craggy rock where it is steep, grass and trees where it is not, snow on Mountain's heights, rounder green ridges on Hawaii. Desert Mountain keeps its one mountain with desert round it, and Seaside Links keeps its sea.

**Course Builder: choose your surroundings.** Under *Landscape* there is a new **Surroundings** block. North, east, south and west can each be one of nine things: as the landscape, open country, rolling hills, a mountain range, one big mountain, mesas, the sea, sea and islands, or a lake. Height and Trees set how tall the land is and how wooded. The choice is saved in the course file and shows in Game view and in the game. A course file made before this version plays exactly as it did.

**Faster on High and Ultra.** A tree used to be drawn either in full or as a very simple far-away copy, so the game drew thousands of trees in full that were only a few millimetres tall on your screen. Each tree now has a middle copy for the distance in between, checked to keep the tree's outline, and shadows far away are cast by the simple copy. On the wooded landscapes the graphics card does a third to a half less work from a tee and from above; on open ones such as Prairie & Sand Hills the gain is smaller. Medium and Low gain from a tee as well. If you had turned the quality down for a big course, try it one step higher.

**Not changed.** Every course, seed and course code gives the same holes as in 0.9.14: none of the ground you play on moved. The ball, the scoring, the challenges and the course of the week are as they were.

**Known.** A course takes about a second longer to load than in 0.9.14. On Low, looking down from very high above a course costs a little more than before.

**Your saves.** Everything carries over from 0.9.14: courses, saved rounds, settings, profiles and friend challenge codes.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
