# Fairway 0.9.6 (beta)

Tag `v0.9.6`, published 2026-10-31.

A second way to make a course, a polished interface, and every hole with a name.

**Architect courses (beta).** In Course studio, *How the course is made* now offers **Architect (beta)** beside Classic. Choose a style -- Golden Age, Links, Sandhills, Parkland, Stadium and seven more, or let the landscape decide -- a club, an age and how much history the land has, and an architect designs the course: every hole has a plan, with drive bunkers where drives come down, a green whose wrong side is guarded, fairways that widen where you land and narrow toward the green, and some of golf's classic holes -- a Redan, an Eden, a Biarritz, a Cape, a punchbowl. Classic is still the default, and every other mode plays Classic courses.

**An Architect course is a place.** Cart paths run down every hole, with little bridges over the creeks; a ball that stops on one gets free relief. A clubhouse in the landscape's own style stands by the first tee, and the nines come home to it. Walls and hedgerows cross the old fields, with a farmstead, a ruined chapel or a lighthouse to aim at; some holes are made by the land -- a blind Dell over a dune, an Alps, a ravine to carry. Sheep, deer, geese and herons keep out of play, a fourball walks the paths far off, and a greenkeeper mows the rough. Ponds are staked, bunkers raked, and yardage posts mark 100, 150 and 200 yards.

**Tournament week.** Choose a Championship venue and the course is dressed for it: grandstands, a scoreboard with you and your group among today's leaders, scores live as you hole out, TV towers, marquees and a crowd that grows from Thursday to a full house on Sunday.

**A yardage book.** Pause any Architect round, or press *Yardage book* in Course studio, for a PDF with a page a hole: the hole drawn tee to green, the carries to every hazard, what is left after a good drive, and the architect's note.

**Every hole has a name, and the flag says where the cup is.** "The Cape", "Long Pond", "Elbow" -- and the last hole is always "Home". Red flags for a front pin, white for the middle, blue for the back. The final scorecard carries the course's crest.

**A polished interface.** Real landscape pictures on the cards and a picture for every saved course; a shorter way into every mode, with *Tee off* on every step and *Play again* at the end; Course studio in five menus; text that reads over the course and bigger buttons to touch. On a phone the round has one bar -- Menu, Map, Views and Fly -- and you can fly the camera with a finger.

**Heather to the horizon.** On the North Sea Links and Haunted Hollow's moor the heather no longer fades to bare purple ground in the distance. Tussocks, fallen logs, sorrel and the Lakeshore Dunes' junipers and driftwood are drawn whole.

**Smoother ground where two holes meet.** A bank could form where one hole's tee area ran into another hole's fairway; it is gone. That changes the ground slightly on courses where holes lie close, so a round saved in 0.9.5 will tell you its course has changed. Your profiles, settings and medals carry over. A friend's challenge code is tied to the version it was made on, so make sure you both have 0.9.6.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open (on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click and choose *Open*).
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser, or the second link it prints on a phone or tablet.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html**.

This is a beta, and Architect courses are a beta inside it: if something looks wrong, *Report a problem* in the help copies what we need.
