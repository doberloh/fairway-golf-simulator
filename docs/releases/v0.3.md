# Fairway 0.3 (beta)

Tag `v0.3`, published 2026-10-07.

The third beta of Fairway.

**New: player profiles.** The first time Fairway opens it asks your name, and from your first shot it keeps your stats, your round history and a sim handicap under **My profile** on the main menu.

- Every golfer is a profile. When you set up a group -- a round, an endless run or the driving range -- each player picks their profile from a list, or **New player...** to add someone. Players can join or leave mid-round, newcomers included.
- The **main profile** is player 1 in every new group. Any profile can be made main.
- Stats: shots, sand shots, bunkers, water, out of bounds, trees and houses hit, chip-ins, lip-outs, mulligans, ball travel and hang time; longest drive, longest putt holed and fastest ball speed; every score from holes in one to triples, par 3/4/5 averages, putts per hole, fairways and greens in regulation.
- **Round history** lists every round with a **Replay this course** button. An endless run replays from its first hole.
- **Sim handicap**: worked out the way the World Handicap System works out a Handicap Index, from your best 8 of your last 20 finished 9 or 18 hole rounds. Fairway's courses have never been rated, so each is rated from its length alone.
- Profiles are kept in your browser on this device. The server's link, Fairway.html opened directly, and a phone each keep their own.

**Ball flight refitted** against Trackman's tour averages and two launch monitor sessions (a GC3 and an R50). Carry, height and landing now track real shots more closely, from driver to wedge.

**Fixed**

- The top menu opened underneath the Shot controls panel, so Play, Course studio and Main menu could not be clicked during a round.
- The Shot controls panel's title bar slipped to the bottom after the panel was moved.
- Replaying a shot in an endless run after the first hole left the game unplayable.
- The shot tracer now trails just behind the ball instead of covering it.

**Getting started**

1. Unzip the whole download into a normal folder.
2. Run **run_fairway_server** and keep its window open. The first time, Windows and macOS warn about a program from the internet: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac, Control-click it and choose *Open*.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:1922` in your browser. On a phone or tablet, open the second link the window prints.

No launch monitor? Just open **Fairway.html** from the download. `Fairway-source.zip` is the source code, and `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` lists each file's checksum.

This is a beta: it hasn't been tried with every launch monitor or every device, and a weak graphics card may struggle even on the lowest setting.
