# maple: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_broadleaf.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_broadleaf/maple-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: Lineup (4,900 tris): a round lumpy ball on a bare stick; the pictures are an upright dense egg with a fine even edge and the leader running into it. Next: leader to 0.78 H, crown an egg of rings of small even lobes widest below the middle, crown starting at 0.27 H.
- r2: Reads as itself: a dense upright egg, fine even edge, leader into the crown, distinct from every oak. Holds; small check next round that the top stays narrower than the foot.
- r3: Holds: the dense upright egg on a clear leader, fine even edge; the only crown of the four with no gaps. Watching that the oak does not converge on it.
- r4: Holds: dense upright egg, fine edge, clear leader; nothing else in the game has this smooth egg.
- r5: Holds: dense upright egg on a clear leader.
- r6: Holds as a dense egg; cost check: 5,144 triangles, over the 4,888 it shipped at. Next: 30 edge lobes (two fewer) and its two low limbs as two-segment twigs.
- r7: Holds after the cost cut (4,780, shipped 4,890): a dense upright egg, fine even edge, the leader under it -- the only gapless crown of the four.
- r8: Autumn: a solid red egg, the picture's red maple at a glance; colour right. Holds.
- r9: Far twins (775, shipped 913): the egg holds with a slightly faceted edge; still the densest outline. Holds.
- r10: Final, summer: a dense upright egg with a fine even edge on a clear leader -- the one gapless smooth crown among the broadleaves.
