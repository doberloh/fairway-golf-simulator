# montereypine: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_calcoast.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_calcoast/montereypine-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a pole with a few flat pads -- wrong species entirely. Pictures: a dense dark irregular dome/egg, foliage to the lower third, coarse upward tufts, heavy short trunk. Next: whorls of heavy limbs each ending in upright tuft masses, outline an irregular egg tapering to a blunt top.
- r2: Whorls of tuft masses: no longer a pole, but now a narrow blocky column of big faceted blobs -- reads as an Italian cypress. Pictures 2 and 4: a broad dense dome nearly as wide as tall. Next: spread 20, smaller, more numerous masses, lowest whorl lower.
- r3: Narrow still -- the spread change had not been made, and two stacked top balls give it a spire tip. Reads as a fir. Next: spread 20 (done now), one wide blunt top lobe.
- r4: Wide now, a dense dark mass to a fifth of the height, but the trunk pokes out of its top like a mast and the masses are big faceted blocks. Next: trunk ends inside the crown (limbs clamped to it), four smaller lobes a limb.
- r5: Trunk now ends inside the crown: a broad, dense, dark, blunt-topped pyramid from a fifth of the height, as picture 2; reads as a big pine apart from the cypress shelves and the oak dome. Lobes still a little faceted. Holds; spring next.
- r6: Spring: dark green, a broad blunt pyramid; lobes still a little faceted but read as one dense mass. Holds.
- r7: Autumn: unchanged dark pyramid. Holds.
- r8: Winter #385a32: unchanged. Holds.
- r9: Far: the twin keeps the broad blunt pyramid (2,600). Count 5,470, over. Next (cost): three bigger lobes a limb instead of four, hidden limbs four-sided.
- r10: Cost: 4,200 triangles. Three bigger lobes a limb leave gaps -- the outline is still the broad pyramid, but the trunk shows through the middle where picture 2 is solid. Next: a core of four lobes up the trunk.
- r11: Trunk core: now a solid broad blunt pyramid of dark masses as picture 2, the trunk hidden. Reads as the Monterey pine, apart from the cypress's shelves. Holds.
- r12: Holds as the solid blunt pyramid; the lobe edges are jagged and faceted, so the outline looks crumpled. Next: less jitter on the lobes.
- r13: Less jitter: the lobes are rounder and the crown reads as one solid blunt dark pyramid, as picture 2. Holds.
- r14: Winter: holds.
- r15: Far: twin 2,310 against 4,360, same solid blunt pyramid. Holds.
- r16: Final: a solid broad blunt-topped dark pyramid of heavy masses from a fifth of the height, short trunk, leaning inland -- picture 2's Monterey pine, apart from the cypress shelves and the oak dome. Done.
