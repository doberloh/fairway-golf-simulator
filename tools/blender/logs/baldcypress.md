# baldcypress: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_southoaks.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_southoaks/baldcypress-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: the column of drooping skirts reads as a fir or hemlock; the pictures show a huge bell-flared fluted foot and an open flat-topped crown of horizontal limbs with feathery sprays. Next: much wider buttress, open level limbs with flat feathery pads, flat spreading top on _1.
- r2: open now with a flat-topped column on _1 and a soft cone on _2 and the flared foot shows; but the pads read as flat maple-leaf stars, not feathery. Next: deeper, droopier, more-pointed pads (hanging fringes), foot flared wider.
- r3: deeper pads still read as flat maple-leaf stars seen from above. Next: pads as five long drooping tassels (fewer points, deep, thin), smaller and two or three a limb, so the crown hangs in feathery sprays.
- r4: drooping five-point tassels still read as leafy hands; skirts will not do here. Next (rebuilt crown): flat feathery sprays -- lumps flattened and stretched along each limb, turned to it and tipped down at the end -- layered up the column, a flat cap of five on the old tree.
- r5: the flattened sprays read as flat layered horizontal foliage -- the cypress at last, nothing like the broadleaf pads -- but too few and thin: the young one is a bare pole. Next: 16 and 14 limbs, three sprays on long limbs, bigger and thicker.
- r6: _1 now reads as a bald cypress: a flared fluted foot, a straight trunk and a narrow column of layered horizontal sprays with a flat top. _2 (young cone) still sparse low down; next 16 limbs and a little wider.
- r7: spring lime #9cc06a reads feathery-fresh on the layered sprays. _2 with 16 limbs is a little fuller but still thin low down; widen its lower sprays next.
- r8: autumn #b4592e rusty orange layered sprays, as picture 04 of the study; _2 with wider low limbs is now a full soft cone of layered sprays, apart from the fir's dark solid spire. Holds.
- r9: winter drawn in summer leaf as the brief says (bare variant later); colour only. Holds.
- r10: far twins drop the limbs and keep the layered sprays: still a flared column of shelves. But the full model is 8,429 / 7,709 triangles -- 51 sprays at 144 each. Next: sprays drawn as a cheap 8 x 5 sphere of my own (64 triangles; 20 in the far twin).
- r11: cost round: sprays as an 8 x 5 sphere of my own (lowball): 4,349 / 4,029 from 8,429 / 7,709, and the sheet shows no visible loss -- the flattened sprays never needed the round outline. Holds.
- r12: holds after the cost round: flared fluted foot, straight trunk, layered horizontal sprays, flat top on _1, soft cone on _2.
- r13: spring with the cheaper sprays and _1's top cap pushed out (0.46 of the half-width, sprays 0.48): the old tree's flat top now spreads like picture 02's umbrella over the layered column; the 8 x 5 sprays read as flat lime layers. Holds.
- r14: far twins with the cheap sprays at 20 triangles each and no limbs: still the layered column with the flat spreading top and the flared foot. Holds.
- r15: final, summer: bell-flared fluted foot, straight tapering trunk, a column of flat layered drooping sprays; _1 flat-topped with a spreading cap, _2 a soft open cone. 4,349 / 4,029 triangles.
