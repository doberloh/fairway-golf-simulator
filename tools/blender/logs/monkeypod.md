# monkeypod: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_hawaii.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_hawaii/monkeypod-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: r1 first build: umbrella outline right (far wider than tall, flat top, low rim) but the canopy is separate lily-pad discs with sky between; the photos are one dense dome. Both variants identical. Next: bigger overlapping lobes, rings closer, skew larger on _2, pink puffs smaller.
- r2: Now one dense umbrella, far wider than tall -- reads as monkeypod, not live oak. But the rim ring hangs like a skirt and hides the trunk, and the squashed lobes read as flat plates (a layered cake). Next: rim raised to half height so trunk and limbs show, rounder lobes on top.
- r3: Rim lifted: the umbrella sits on a stub of trunk with limbs fanning under it -- silhouette reads (a mushroom far wider than tall). Rim lobes still plate-like and the trunk too short to see. Next: trunk to a quarter of the height, rim lobes rounder.
- r4: Taller trunk now shows under the umbrella; outline reads as monkeypod (dome far wider than tall, dark underside, limbs). Rim lobes too even, a scalloped ring. Next: rim heights jittered.
- r5: Jittered rim breaks the scallop ring; umbrella on a visible trunk with dark underside reads as monkeypod next to photos 2-3, and nothing else in the game is this wide and flat. Holds.
- r6: Spring: lighter yellow-green #7a9a4a reads as new flush; the pink puffs are too small to see at this size, as in the photos (kept small on purpose). Shape holds. 4,700 triangles.
- r7: Autumn #5a843c, no puffs (accent drawn leaf). Holds.
- r8: Winter #6a8a42 (spec: thinner) reads paler; shape holds. The thinner winter canopy is a later variant.
- r9: Far twins (1,700 triangles, 37%) keep the umbrella outline exactly; lobes a little faceted, invisible at far distance. Holds.
- r10: Unchanged, holds. Cost count: 4,700 / 4,600, far 1,750 / 1,660; the 18 pink puffs cost 360 for little -- next cut to 12.
- r11: Cost round: 12 puffs (-120 triangles). Against photo 2 the top is a little too round -- the spec says flat-topped. Next: upper rings higher and flatter.
- r12: Upper rings raised and flattened: the top is now flat like photo 2, the umbrella far wider than tall with a ruffled low rim and limbs between. Up close the rim lobes show facets (10-sided). Next: 12-sided rim lobes.
- r13: 12-sided rim lobes smooth the ruffled edge; flat-topped umbrella holds against photos 2-3.
- r14: Holds.
- r15: Final, summer: flat-topped umbrella about twice as wide as tall on a short trunk, limbs fanning under a low ruffled rim -- reads as monkeypod against photos 2-3 and apart from the live oak's lumpy dome. 4,990 / 4,860 triangles, far 1,750 / 1,660.
