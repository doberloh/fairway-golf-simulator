# wiregrass: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_southpines.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_southpines/wiregrass-r<round>.png (local).

- r3: Arching fine hair, but so fine it nearly vanishes at the sheet's size. Next: blades a bit wider and more of them.
- r4: Wider blades read now as a fine arching tuft; the pictures' tufts are a touch rounder and fuller. Holds; next looks at its colour in spring.
- r5: Holds in shape, but 2,100 triangles for a grass tuft is too dear. Next: half the blades (about 1,000), still three tufts.

- r7: Holds: fine arching tuft.
- r8: Holds: fine arching tuft. Next: spring colour.
