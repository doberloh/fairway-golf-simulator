# pacificrhododendron: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_pacific.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_pacific/pacificrhododendron-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: five stems, a pom-pom of leaves and one truss each. Pom-poms on sticks; the real shrub is leggy but leafy in whorls, several pink trusses. Next: eight stems, fuller leaf whorls, three small pink trusses a stem.
- r2: Eight stems, leaf whorls, pink trusses: reads as the shrub; a little too leggy and thin in leaf beside the pictures. Next: bigger whorls, a lower set of leaves.
- r3: Bigger whorls, a lower set: it stacked into topiary on one stem. Next: stems splay from the base, whorls spread out sideways.
- r4: Stems splayed from the base: leggy with whorls and pink trusses, like the first picture. Next: a few more leaves low down.
- r5: More low leaves: leggy and leafy with pink trusses, near the second picture. Next: hold.
- r6: Held: leggy, leafy, pink trusses. Next: judge it out of flower.
- r7: Spring: pink trusses on dark leaves, as the second picture. Next: out of flower.
- r8: Out of flower: a leggy evergreen shrub; reads. Next: hold.
- r9: Summer: leggy and evergreen. Next: hold.
- r10: Scale: 4 m against 2-6 m -- right; heavy for a shrub. Next: coarser lobes.
- r11: Spring: leggy, leafy, pink, as the first picture; 9,384 faces. Next: six stems, coarser whorls.
- r12: The cost pass: 1,236 faces, from 9,384 -- the whorls went flat and wide, a stack of lily pads; the pictures are a leafy bush. Next: rounder, smaller whorls.
- r13: Rounder whorls: less like lily pads but still top-heavy -- a crown of umbrellas on bare stems; the pictures carry leaves down the stems. Next: a second whorl partway up every stem.
- r14: A second whorl on every stem: leaves run down the bush now, no longer umbrellas on sticks; 1,596 faces. Next: spring's trusses.
- r15: Spring, final: leafy down its stems, pink trusses on top, as the first picture; 1,596 faces. Done.
