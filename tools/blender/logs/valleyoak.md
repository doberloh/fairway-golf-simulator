# valleyoak: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_calinland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_calinland/valleyoak-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a thin trunk under a mushroom of flat plates, the weeping fringe drawn as separate blobs dangling like lanterns. The pictures are a massive short trunk under a broad irregular dome, wider than tall, limbs sweeping low, the edge drooping as one ragged hem. Silhouette reads as a mushroom, not a valley oak. Next: trunk half as thick again and shorter, rounder clumps filling down to a third of the height, hanging clumps joined under the limb ends.
- r2: Massive short trunk now, crown fuller -- but it is a compact rounded ball about 1.3 times as wide as tall, and the hanging ends still dangle as separate lanterns. The pictures are far wider than tall (1.6x), limbs sweeping out near level, the lower edge a ragged hem. Silhouette: still not a valley oak. Next: longer reach, lower peak, three clumps a limb with gaps, the hanging piece a tall clump overlapping the end clump.
- r3: Wider now (the second variant near the pictures' proportions) and the hem is joined, but the crown is a dense broccoli head: no limb shows, no sky through it -- still the 'green ball' the owner warned of. The pictures show dark limbs between separate masses. Next: two clumps a limb plus a lower side branch with its own clump, smaller top, thicker limbs.
- r4: Opened up -- gaps and limbs -- but now it reads as separate big balls on sticks, a single hat on top, the hem pendants dominating (row image). The pictures: a ragged fine-edged broad mass, dark limbs inside, a clear bole a quarter of the height. Next: each mass a cluster of small balls (along the arch, side branch, a hanging cluster for the hem), trunk to a quarter of the height.
- r5: Clusters of small balls: a broad ragged mass with a fine edge, the hem hanging at the sides, limbs dark inside -- the second variant now reads like the first picture; the first is still a little tall and centred. Silhouette: broad, ragged, weeping edge -- reads as a valley oak, apart from the live oak's smooth low dome. Next: spring colours.
- r6: Spring: bright young green on the broad ragged mass; the shape holds. Colour per spec.
- r7: Spring again: holds; no change this round.
- r8: Autumn: tan-yellow per spec on the broad ragged weeping mass; holds.
- r9: Winter: drawn in leaf (its bare variant with grey limbs comes later); holds.
- r10: Far round: both far twins keep the broad ragged weeping outline at about half the triangles (2,540 and 2,930). Beside the pictures the upper middle is thin and the second variant's low hem hides its trunk. Next (cost round): fewer balls on the inner clusters, a raised middle mass, the second variant's limb ends less low, six limbs.
- r11: Cost round: 5,130 triangles each (far twins 2,510). The raised middle fills the upper centre and the second variant's trunk shows again under a broad ragged weeping crown -- reads as a valley oak beside all three pictures; not the live oak's smooth low dome nor the bur oak's flat shelf.
- r12: Summer recheck after the cost round: holds.
- r13: Spring final check: bright young leaf over the broad ragged weeping crown; holds.
- r14: Summer: holds (5,130 triangles).
- r15: Far round: both far twins (2,510 triangles) keep the broad ragged crown, raised middle, weeping hem and visible trunk of their models; holds.
- r16: Summer, final (same build as rounds 14-15): a short massive trunk, long limbs arching out and down, a broad ragged crown of small-ball clusters with a raised middle and a weeping hem at the sides; 5,130 triangles, far twin 2,510. Reads as a valley oak by silhouette. Done for the pack; the bare winter variant is owed.
