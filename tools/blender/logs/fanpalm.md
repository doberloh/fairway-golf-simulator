One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_deserts/fanpalm-r<round>.png (local).

- r2: Bigger fans and skirt: the head now as wide as the skirt; still a little small against the pictures' heavy heads. Next: check the head at row scale, fans a little bigger.
- r3: Seen at row scale: the head is a dense green broccoli ball and the skirt a smooth narrow sleeve; the pictures show spiky split fans with hanging tips and a ragged skirt as wide as the head. Next: fans with deep splits and hanging tips, fewer but bigger; the skirt wider, ridged, with a ragged hem.
