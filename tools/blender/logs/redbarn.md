# redbarn: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_heartland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_heartland/redbarn-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: the gambrel reads but the roof is a heavy dark hood over short walls, no white trim, and a white silo that looks like a column. The pictures: red walls, white trim on every edge and door, a concrete silo taller and slimmer with a grey dome. Next: spec size 20 x 12 m, trim along gables, corners and doors, hay door, windows, a cupola, a tan silo 16 m high.
- r2: Size, white trim, doors, hay door, windows, cupola, tan silo: the front now reads like the second picture. The silo is too pale (cream, not grey concrete) and the barn alone is plain beside the pictures' lean-to sheds. Next: a darker banded silo, a lean-to on one side.
- r3: Banded grey-tan silo and a lean-to shed: the group now reads like the second and third pictures. The gambrel's knee is soft from the sheet's high camera. Next: a little more break at the knee, hold the rest.
- r4: Knee sharpened (lower pitch steeper, inset 1.1 m): a touch more gambrel on the roof's side; the front reads as the pictures. Holds. Next: check proportions against the second picture once more.
- r5: Held: the front, trim, lean-to and banded silo read as the second picture's group. Next: winter check of the colours (fixed through the year).
- r6: Spring: colours fixed through the year (red, white, grey); holds. Next: autumn.
- r7: Autumn: unchanged colours; the roof grey picks up the warm light. Holds. Next: winter.
- r8: Winter: unchanged; holds. Next: cost.
- r9: No far twin (750 triangles all told). Seen at this size the front gable and the far gambrel silhouette both read; holds. Next: hold.
- r10: Held, 750 triangles. The pictures' gable ends carry a little peaked hood over the hay door. Next: add the hood.
- r11: Hay hood added over the hay door (grey, peaked, 1 m out); against the grey roof behind it it barely shows from the sheet's high camera, but it gives the gable end its shadow line from the ground. 770 triangles. Next: hold.
- r12: Spring: the silo comes out a cool grey-white here where every picture's concrete is warm tan-grey. Next: warmer, darker silo paint.
- r13: Warmer, darker silo paint (concrete tan, brown-grey hoops): now the warm tan-grey of the pictures' silos rather than white. Next: summer final.
- r14: Held (no twin); hay hood and silo in place, 770 triangles. Next: summer final.
- r15: Summer, final: red gambrel barn with white trim, X doors, hay door and hood, cupola, lean-to, banded concrete silo -- the second picture's farm. 770 triangles. Done; holds.
