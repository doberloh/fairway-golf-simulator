# lupine: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_mountain.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_mountain/lupine-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: five spikes over a few leaf lumps. The spikes read; too few, and the leaves are lost. Next: eight spikes, a fuller palmate base.
- r2: Eight spikes, a fuller base: reads as lupine, but lost beside the 1.8 m post. Next: the sheet's small post for plants under 0.9 m.
- r3: The small post: a dense clump of purple spikes over a leaf base, like the first picture. The spikes are a little too fat. Next: slimmer spikes.
- r4: Slimmer flower lumps: hardly changed -- each lump's height, not its width, makes the spikes fat. Next: flatter lumps, a sharper taper.
- r5: Flatter lumps, sharper taper: slim spikes now, but stacked discs with gaps -- a pagoda -- where the pictures are solid columns of florets. Next: twelve florets a spike, closer together.
- r6: Twelve florets a spike: solid columns of flowers now, like the first picture's spikes. Next: spring, out of flower.
- r7: The cost pass: each spike a tapering column with three florets, 900 triangles from 2,160. In spring the spikes are green cones, which reads oddly; the real plant carries tight purple-green buds then. Next: a bud colour for spring, and summer to check the spikes still read as florets.
- r8: Autumn: tan pods on the spikes, as the spec. Next: summer, to see the spikes as florets.
- r9: Summer after the cost pass: the spikes are smooth purple cones -- the florets the pictures show are gone. Next: six florets a spike on five spikes, about 1,100 triangles.
- r10: Six florets on five spikes: the spikes read as florets again, 1,075 triangles (2,160 at round 6). Next: summer to see them in colour.
- r11: Summer: purple spikes with florets proud of them, like the first picture. Next: hold.
- r12: Held; no change.
- r13: Checked the spikes at the sheet's size: florets read. Next: spring buds.
- r14: Spring buds: grey-purple spikes over the leaves, as lupines carry before they open. Next: summer, final.
- r15: Summer, final: purple spikes with florets proud of them over a palmate base, as the first picture; 1,075 triangles. Done.
