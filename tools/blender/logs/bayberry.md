# bayberry: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_neparkland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_neparkland/bayberry-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a tidy rounded dome with tiny grey berries; holds as a coastal shrub, though the berries barely show and the pictures (02-03, beach plum) are twiggier. Next: a slightly ragged edge, berries bigger.
- r2: Bigger grey berry clusters now show; rounded dome with a softer ragged edge. Holds.
- r3: No change to the shape; the berries cost 1,360 triangles, more than the leaves. Next: three berries a cluster, on half the lobes.
- r4: Three berries a cluster on half the lobes; they still read as grey dots. Holds.
- r5: No change; holds.
- r6: Holds; berries down to 660 triangles, 2,130 in all. Next: spring (beach-plum white).
- r7: Spring: white beach-plum blossom in the berry clusters (#f0ece0); reads but sparse beside 02-03's smothered bush -- accepted, the accent stays a few small lumps (budget). Next: autumn.
- r8: Autumn: olive-bronze leaf (#7a6a3a) with purple plums in the berry clusters. Held.
- r9: Winter: duller semi-evergreen leaf, grey berries showing. Held.
- r10: No far twin; no change. Holds.
- r11: Its lobes read the same as the scrub oak's beside it. Next: more, smaller, smoother lobes in four rings -- a tidy fine-leaved dome.
- r12: Finer, smoother lobes in four rings: a tidy dome apart from the scrub oak; but the bottom ring shows gaps between its lobes. Next: a core mass behind them.
- r13: Core mass closed the low gaps: a tidy dense dome on the sand with grey berry clusters, as 01's bayberry. Holds.
- r14: Cost check: about 3,100 triangles with the core and berries, inside the shrub budget. Holds.
- r15: Final, summer: a tidy dense rounded dome of fine lobes with grey waxy berry clusters (white beach-plum blossom in spring). 3,220 triangles.
