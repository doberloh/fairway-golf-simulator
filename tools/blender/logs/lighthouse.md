# lighthouse: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_drylake.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_drylake/lighthouse-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a red tapering tower on a square block, a dark lantern and a red cone. Reads as a generic lighthouse. Looking through the pictures, the most recognisable Great Lakes light is Grand Haven's: a straight red cylinder on a concrete pier, a railed gallery, a lantern of pale glass panes under a red roof and ball vent. Next: that -- a pier-end concrete block, a door, portholes, mullioned glass lantern, railing.
- r2: Grand Haven's light: a straight red tower, railed dark gallery, glass lantern with mullions, red roof and ball vent -- it reads as the second picture at once. The concrete block under it is a tall plinth; the pictures stand the tower on a low pier deck. Next: a lower, longer pier end; the roof a touch broader.
- r3: Lower, longer pier deck and a broader roof: the tower now stands on a deck, not a plinth, and still reads as Grand Haven's. Holds; next round looks at the gallery and lantern proportions.
- r4: Held: Grand Haven's red tower, railed gallery and glass lantern on its pier deck. Compared the shaft's slenderness with the second picture (about four widths to the gallery) -- right. No change.
- r5: Held; no change. The sheet's front view shows the door and portholes facing -Y. Next: its colours in the other seasons (it has none of its own).
- r6: Spring: unchanged (a painted building has no season). Holds.
- r7: Spring, unchanged; holds.
- r8: Held, spring unchanged.
- r9: Summer: holds. 740 triangles, cheap for a building.
- r10: Held; no change.
- r11: Held through autumn. Beside the second picture the gallery sits on the shaft like a plate; Grand Haven's tower flares out under its gallery. Next: a flared red corbel under the gallery deck.
- r12: The flared red corbel under the gallery now carries the deck, as on Grand Haven's tower; 800 triangles. Holds.
- r13: Held; reads as Grand Haven's light beside the second picture at sheet size.
- r14: Spring: unchanged; holds.
- r15: Spring: holds (no change).
- r16: Spring: holds (no change).
- r17: Summer, final: Grand Haven's pierhead light -- a straight red tower flaring under a dark railed gallery, a pale glass lantern with dark mullions, red roof and ball vent, on a 6 x 12 m concrete pier end; about 17 m tall. 800 triangles. Done.
