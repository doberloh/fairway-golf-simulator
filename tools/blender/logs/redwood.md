# redwood: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_redwood.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_redwood/redwood-r<round>.png (local).

- r6: r1-5 on sheets: a trunk with boughs round a solid dark core, then plates, then clumps. The owner: trees are not solid in the middle. Northwood's photographs: bare to a third or half the height, the crown open and wispy, sky through it, the trunk running up inside. r6: rebuilt as it grows: limbs as wood, unevenly spaced, separate hanging sprays, no core (flora-built-from-structure).
- r7: r7: r6 had the structure but a sapling's foliage beside Northwood's. Limbs closer, five sprays a limb fanned to both sides, wider sprays, cheaper lumps.
- r8: r8: reads as a tree at last, but the crown small and thin on a slim pole beside Northwood's: limbs reach further and hold their width higher, sprays larger, trunk thicker (0.027 H).
- r9: r9: fuller but now a broad pine, a third as wide as tall; Northwood's are a sixth to a fifth. Narrower limbs with sprays that keep their size, so a narrow crown stays dense.
- r10: r10: narrow and dense now, close to Northwood's at sheet size. Crown starts lower (0.36 H), four sprays a limb to keep the near model under 5,000 faces, far twin rebuilt to a third of the limbs and two big sprays: the first was 3,370 faces, far too many for a forest.
- r11: r11: the owner: the trunk stays nearly one width all the way up, not how real trees work. In the game too: straight dark columns with thin crowns. Trunk rebuilt to narrow all the way, foot 0.031 H, 0.57 of that at mid-height, nothing at the tip, a small root flare; limbs thin going up. Far twin given more foliage: the woods read as poles at a distance.
- r12: r12: in the game from the tee the sprays read as big flat leaves. Near model's sprays become toothed fronds with a rib and a sag; the far twin keeps lumps. Bark lighter, redder.
- r13: r13: fronds read finely; near model at 6,500+ faces, so fewer limbs and sprays, wider fronds, to keep the crown's fill.
- r14: r14: in the game the structure holds and the overview reads as a redwood valley, but crowns thinner than Northwood's after r13's cut: limbs back closer, fronds wider. Growth code moved to grown.py for other packs.
- r15: r15: beside Northwood's photographs and in the game: the tapered trunk, the open crown with sky through it and the bare lower bole hold. Kept.
- r16: r16: the owner: the skeletons are right, the foliage too blocky. Lumps and toothed plates thrown out: foliage is now stems of thin pointed leaflets in pairs, swept forward, sagging, dark at the root and light at the tip, seen from both sides, far twins too.
- r17: r17: in the game fine and feathery at last, and close to the misty Northwood photograph, but crowns more see-through than the sunlit ones: wider sprays, one more a limb, limbs a little closer. Six pairs of leaflets a spray to pay for it.
- r18: r18: 117 million triangles a frame on High, four times Puget Sound: the far twin's 2,270 faces across thousands of trees. Far twin cut to about 400: wide sprays of few leaflets on a third of the limbs, no limb wood. Near model kept.
