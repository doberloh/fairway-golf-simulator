# noblefir: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_westmtn.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_westmtn/noblefir-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: separate whorls of stiff up-angled brushes -- reads as a bottlebrush; the domed top and cones are too small to show. Next: spread 7 m; check the top and cones on the row image.
- r2: A narrow bulging bottlebrush -- too cylindrical; the pictures show a conical spire whose stiff tiers sweep up, the old tree's crown high on a bare trunk. Next: crown from 40%, taper to the top, brushes swept up more.
- r3: Conical now, brushes swept up, the crown on the upper 60% of a bare grey trunk; reads as a stiff-tiered fir apart from the red fir's flat shelves. Holds; cones checked in spring.
- r4: Holds; its 55 thin limbs cost 1,100 triangles of bark -- a cost round will drop them from the far twin.
- r5: Spring: pale blue-green new tips #6f9a8a; the young purple cones are too few and small to show at sheet size. Holds; cones checked at full size.
- r6: Unchanged; holds.
- r7: Autumn: cones brown #8a6a44 on the top whorls; holds. Its 55 thin branch tubes are 1,100 triangles of bark. Next: the cost round, thin branches left out of the far twin.
- r8: Cost round: thin branch tubes left out of the far twin, its bark 1,056 to 66 triangles, the brushes floating unseen at that size. Picture 4 shows the crown nearly to the ground; ours keeps a bare trunk (picture 3) but lower, from 32%, 12 whorls.
- r9: Crown from 32% on 12 whorls: a tall narrow tufted spire with a domed top, like pictures 1 and 4. Holds.
- r10: Winter #45706a, cones off (leaf colour) as they shatter on the tree. Holds.
- r11: Unchanged; holds.
- r12: Holds.
- r13: Holds.
- r14: Holds.
- r15: Counted 4,230 (far twin 1,160). Holds.
- r16: Far twin keeps the tufted spire without its thin branches. Holds.
- r17: Final, summer: a tall narrow spire of separate stiff up-swept blue-green brushes, domed top with upright cones, bare grey trunk below a third. 4,230 triangles.
