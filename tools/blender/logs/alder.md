# alder: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_broadleaf.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_broadleaf/alder-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: Lineup (3,800 tris): an egg on a stick, smooth, the same as the maple but taller. Pictures: a narrow pointed-oval open crown, a grey stem through it. Next: nine loose clumps up the stem on short limbs, widest a third up, pointed top.
- r2: Pointed outline right, but nine small clumps stack up the pole like a pagoda. Next: eleven clumps a third bigger, overlapping, crown down to 0.32 H so it is a full pointed oval with a few gaps.
- r3: Overcorrected to a pointed column -- a cypress/conifer spire, the two upright top lobes worst. Next: clumps further out (wider, 0.3 floor), random reach so gaps open, one blunt top lobe -- a pointed oval, not a spire.
- r4: Better (blunt top, a limb in a gap) but still narrow enough to read as a conifer spire beside the pictures' fuller oval. Next: width up a fifth (10.5 m on 20 m) so it is a pointed oval of loose clumps.
- r5: Wider now and open at the sides, but widest at the foot so it reads as a pear / a conifer's skirt. Next: widest at 0.4 of the crown, tapering to a blunt point -- an oval.
- r6: Reads as a pointed oval of loose clumps now, a grey stem through it -- not a spire, not the maple's dense egg. Cost: 3,925 (shipped 3,831) and the far twin 1,005 (shipped 603), the eleven branches most of it. Next: a twig on every third clump only, trunk 8 sides.
- r7: Reads as an alder: a slender grey stem through a pointed oval of big loose clumps with gaps, wider and lower than the aspen, coarser and more open than the maple. 3,510, far 590 (shipped 3,830 and 600). Holds; next autumn.
- r8: Autumn: olive-brown (red alder drops its leaves nearly green); the oval holds. Holds.
- r9: Far twins (590, shipped 603): pointed oval of loose clumps holds, the stem visible in the gaps. Holds.
- r10: Final, summer: a slender grey stem through a pointed oval of big loose clumps, gaps showing the stem -- wider and lower than the aspen, coarser than the maple.
