# madrone: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_pacific.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_pacific/madrone-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: two or three leaning red stems under one round cloud crown. Beside the pictures the crown is a tidy lollipop, high and centred; real madrones sprawl, the crown broken into clumps on twisting red limbs that show through it. Next: wider spread, the crown as clumps on the limb ends, more twist.
- r2: Clumps on red limb ends: the limbs read now, but it swung too sparse -- a V of two stems with small tufts, where the pictures are a broad dense crown with red limbs showing through. Next: four limbs a stem, clumps half as big again, a broad upper layer joining them.
- r3: Bigger clumps and an upper layer: one crown now, red limbs showing, but its top is a flat table -- an umbrella pine -- where madrone crowns are deep and irregularly domed. Next: clumps taller, set at more varied heights, the upper layer rounder.
- r4: Deeper clumps, a domed top: no longer a table, but the stems lean so far apart the crown splits in two with a gap down the middle; the pictures are one broad crown. Next: stems lean less, a central clump fills the middle.
- r5: Stems lean less, a central clump: one crown now with red stems under it, like the first picture. Too narrow for the broad spreading madrones of the second. Next: spread 20% wider, so the red limbs show between clumps.
- r6: Spread 20% wider: broad now, but the crown is so dense the red limbs never show, where every picture shows them between the leaf masses. Next: fewer lobes a clump, a smaller centre, so gaps open.
- r7: Spring colours, fewer lobes a clump: red stems clear under a dark evergreen crown, but the top is still flat -- every clump sits at the same height. The pictures dome in the middle. Next: limbs pointing up the stem's line reach higher, the outer ones lower.
- r8: Limbs along the lean reach higher: still a flat top -- the stems' own lean carries every high limb outward, so nothing rises in the middle -- and autumn shows none of the orange-red berries the spec names. Next: two or three clumps raised over the centre, berries in the accent role over the crown's skin.
- r9: Raised centre clumps, berries: the far twin keeps the silhouette, but the top is still a slab -- the broad flattened upper layer from round 3 is what makes it, and the raised clumps sink into it. Next: drop the upper layer; the clumps and the raised centre make the crown.
- r10: Upper layer dropped: the top domes at last, lumpy, on red limbs. It still sits high on bare stems; the pictures run limbs through a lower, broader crown. Next: fork lower, five longer limbs a stem, slightly smaller clumps.
- r11: Lower fork, five longer limbs, smaller clumps: the red limbs show between the clumps and spring's white flowers dot the crown -- close to the second picture. But 9,268 faces is twice a tree's budget (the cartoon oak is about 5,100 triangles). Next: the cost pass.
- r12: The cost pass: three or four limbs a stem, four lobes a clump -- 5,806 faces, from 9,268. The domed, open crown on red limbs survives, a little thinner. Next: check it in autumn with its berries.
- r13: Autumn after the cost pass: orange-red berries over the evergreen crown, red limbs between the clumps -- what the spec asks. Next: winter and the far twin.
- r14: Winter and the far twins: evergreen with berries, as the spec; both twins keep the open domed crown and red limbs in a few hundred faces. Next: spring once more.
- r15: Spring, final: the open domed evergreen crown on twisting red limbs, white flower clusters, 5,806 faces with a far twin -- beside the second and third pictures it reads as a madrone. Done for the pack; its colours by season go into the game's species tables.
- r18: Second cost pass, after the game's A/B (Puget Sound's Low frame +12% with the pack, triangles +18%): Blender's face counts had hidden the triangles -- the three-stem madrone was 9,800, the region's pine 3,400. Three limbs a stem, three-lobe clumps, two raised clumps: 6,700 for three stems; the two-stem variant, 4,600, now ships. The domed open crown on red limbs holds on the sheet.
