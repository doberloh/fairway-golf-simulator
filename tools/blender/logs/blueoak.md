# blueoak: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_calinland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_calinland/blueoak-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a long thin trunk and a few big flat polygon puffs -- an acacia's umbrella. The pictures: a short pale trunk under a compact rounded crown about as tall as wide, small clumps. Next: shorter thicker trunk, crown from a third of the height, more and smaller clusters at more heights.
- r2: Short trunk now, but the crown is separate polygon discs stacked tall, reading close to the olive. The pictures: a cohesive rounded crown on one short straight trunk. Next: bigger clusters of more balls, the top lower, so the crown is one rounded mass with a lumpy edge.
- r3: One crown now, but its few big faceted balls read coarse, a generic round tree. Next: more, smaller balls in each cluster, so the edge is fine and lumpy.
- r4: Finer balls but they still render as faceted polygon blobs. Next: smoother 8 x 5 balls in the clusters.
- r5: Smoother balls: little change -- the merged balls light as one blob. It reads as a small rounded lumpy oak like the second picture, blue-grey; the low clusters raised a little for a rounder crown.
- r6: Spring: fresh green over the pale trunk, compact rounded crown; holds.
- r7: Spring again: holds.
- r8: Autumn: tan crown; holds.
- r9: Winter: drawn in leaf, blue-grey; holds.
- r10: Far round (no twin, under 15 m): counted 6,390 and 5,070 triangles -- over a tree's budget. Next: fewer, cheaper balls.
- r11: Cost round: 3,330 and 2,670 triangles. A small rounded lumpy blue-grey crown on one short pale trunk, as tall as wide -- apart from the olive's low wide mop on split stems. Holds.
- r12: Summer recheck: holds.
- r13: Spring final check: holds.
- r14: Summer: holds.
- r15: Far round (no twin): the compact rounded blue-grey crown on one short trunk holds; at a thumbnail it stays apart from the olive by its single Y-forked trunk and taller crown.
- r16: Summer, final: a compact rounded lumpy blue-grey crown, as tall as wide, on one short pale Y-forked trunk; 3,330 and 2,670 triangles. Done; bare winter variant owed.
