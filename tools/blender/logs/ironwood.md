# ironwood: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_drylake.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_drylake/ironwood-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: two or three thin trunks under one solid cloud. Beside the pictures it is a lollipop -- real ironwoods have short heavy split trunks forking low into crooked spreading limbs, and a broad open dome wider than tall with limbs showing through, its edges drooping. Next: heavy short trunks, crooked limbs out to clumps on their ends, outer clumps lower.
- r2: Clumps on limb ends: no lollipop now, but it swung to an acacia -- flat discs on long thin bare limbs with sky everywhere, where the pictures are a broad dense dome with the crooked limbs showing only underneath. Next: clumps bigger and rounder, a second ring of clumps part-way out each limb, heavier limbs.
- r3: Mid-limb clumps fill it: a dense crown now, but a mushroom -- narrow, high on a tall leg, its underside level. The pictures' dome is twice as wide as it is tall at the rim and hangs low at the edges. Next: spread 8.5 -> 10 m, outer clumps much lower, shorter trunks leaning out more.
- r4: Wider (10 m) with the outer clumps low: a broad dome now, rim drooping, closer to the first picture. But the trunks are thin for the first picture's heavy split bole, and the smooth big lobes have none of the crown's fine leaflet texture. Next: trunks a third thicker, three small flakes out on every clump's skin (leaflets in summer, the lavender bloom in May).
- r5: Thicker trunks: the heavy split bole of the first picture reads now. The leaflet flakes vanished -- set inside the lobes they never reach the skin. Next: the flakes just outside each clump, and a spring round to see the bloom.
- r6: Spring: lavender flakes now sit on every clump's skin, but they are a sprinkle, where the first picture's crown is lavender-pink all over in May. The spec's spring colour (#8a96a8) is the bloom over the grey-green, i.e. the leaf itself. Next: spring leaf #8a96a8 with the lavender accent over it.
- r7: Spring leaf #8a96a8 (the spec's): the crown turns blue-grey, a blue spruce's colour, where the first picture's bloom is a warm lavender-pink haze over the green. Decided: depart from the spec's hex for a warmer mauve-grey (#a49aa6) with a pinker accent (#c8a8d8). Next: summer, to check the form.
- r8: Mauve-grey spring leaf with pink-lavender flakes: now the first picture's blooming haze rather than a blue conifer's colour. Both variants are the same dense dome, though; the second and third pictures are more open, the crooked limbs showing through. Next: the smaller variant's clumps 18% smaller, so its limbs show (the first stays the dense one), and the far twins.
- r9: Far twins: both keep the broad dome and split trunks in a third of the triangles. The smaller variant now shows its limbs under a lighter crown, closer to the second picture. But counted: the full tree is 6,400 triangles (budget 2,000-5,000), and in the twin the leaflet flakes are half its cost. Next, the cost pass: no mid clump on a stem's middle limb, one-lobe centre clumps, two flakes a clump, the twin keeping one flake in three.
- r10: Cost pass: 5,160 and 4,690 triangles (from 6,400 and 5,700), the far twins 1,260 and 1,120. The crown is a little more open and still broad, but its clumps read as flat stacked pads, a juniper bonsai's, where the first picture's dome is continuous. Next: rounder clumps (taller by a sixth), at no cost; autumn colours.
- r11: Autumn (evergreen, unchanged colour) with rounder clumps: the crown now reads as one lumpy continuous dome over the split trunks, not stacked pads -- nearer the first picture. Holds. Next: winter.
- r12: Winter (#7a8a6c, evergreen, a touch duller): unchanged form, holds. Next: the far twins again after the rounder clumps.
- r13: Far twins after the rounder clumps: both keep the broad dome and the split trunks (1,260 and 1,120 triangles against 5,160 and 4,690). Holds. Next: spring once more.
- r14: Spring again: the mauve-grey crown with pink-lavender flakes over the rounder clumps reads as the first picture's tree in bloom. Holds. Next: summer, final.
- r15: Spring: holds (no change to the tree this round).
- r16: Spring: holds (no change).
- r17: Summer, final: short heavy split trunks forking low into crooked limbs under a broad lumpy grey-green dome of clumps, wider than tall, its rim hanging low; the second variant smaller and more open with its limbs showing. Beside the first and third pictures it reads as an ironwood. 5,160 and 4,690 triangles, far twins 1,260 and 1,120. Done.
- r18: Silhouette lineup (the owner: trees read as green balls on trunks): round 17's crown is a handful of big tidy round clumps on the limb ends -- reads as bonsai, nothing like the pictures' broad, open, ragged twiggy crown with sky all through it. Next: limbs forking into crooked twigs, the crown as many small ragged sprays (a coarse lobe and two flakes) on the twig tips, spaced apart, the rim drooping.
- r19: Silhouette: forked crooked limbs under small ragged sprays, sky through it -- reads as an open desert tree now, no longer bonsai. But too sparse, too low and flat (the sprays are pads, like an acacia), and the second variant's middle sprays float with no limb. Next: the crown raised, the sprays less flat, a loose flake part-way along every twig to fill without closing, the middle sprays on limbs; the near lobes 80-face where they are big (20-face icosahedra read faceted).
- r20: Silhouette: a broad open dome of small ragged sprays on forked crooked limbs, sky between, the crown higher -- reads as a desert ironwood rather than bonsai. The rim does not droop yet, and the two middle limbs stand straight up like poles. Next: a flake hanging under each outer spray (lower the farther out), twigs reaching a little wider, the middle limbs leaning and bowed; trunk sides 7 to 6 to pay for the flakes.
- r21: Silhouette: the rim now droops -- flakes hang under the outer sprays -- over a broad open ragged crown, crooked limbs showing through; reads as an ironwood beside the second and third pictures. The two middle limbs are as thick as the main limbs and crowd the heart. Next: the middle limbs thinner.
- r22: Spring: the whole open crown mauve-grey with lavender flakes, as the first picture's tree in bloom; the middle limbs now thin twigs, the heart open. The broad, ragged, drooping-rimmed outline with limbs through it holds; no change.
- r23: Autumn (evergreen grey-green, as summer): the open ragged dome with its drooping rim and crooked limbs through it holds. Cost round: the far twin had grown to 4,000 triangles (its flakes cost as much far as near); now one bigger lobe a spray and no flakes there, and one flower flake in four left out near.
