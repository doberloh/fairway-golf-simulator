# americanbeech: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_newengland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_newengland/americanbeech-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: short trunk, level and rising limbs, clumps at the ends: silhouette is separate big balls with sky between -- still 'fluffy balls', and too tall in the trunk, where the third picture is a broad dense low dome. Next: shorter trunk, lower limbs drooping toward the ground, more and smaller clumps closing the dome's outline.
- r2: Shorter trunk, drooping lower limbs, more clumps: still a stack of separate balls on grey limbs -- exactly the owner's 'fluffy balls', and taller than wide where the third picture is a broad dense low mass. Next: wider than tall (spread 22, height 18), a lower ring of overlapping clumps on seven level limbs and an overlapping upper dome, lobes closer than their size so the outline is one lumpy mass.
- r3: Wider than tall now (18 x 22 m), a low ring of clumps on level limbs, trunk showing under it -- the beech's low broad outline. But every lobe still inks as its own ball. Next: lobes a quarter bigger and closer, normals bent fully to the crown (blend 0.9) so the dome lights as one mass.
- r4: Bigger closer lobes, normals bent to the crown: the dome now lights as one broad mass. But the low ring sags to the ground and hides the trunk, so the outline is a big shrub mound -- confusable with a bush. Next: lower limbs and their clumps raised, so the grey trunk shows under a level crown base, as in the second picture.
- r5: Lower ring raised: the short grey trunk shows under a level crown base, the dome broad and low and lit as one mass -- the beech outline, no longer a shrub mound. Holds; next: the seasons.
- r6: Holds: broad low dome lit as one, short grey trunk under a level base; 4,500 triangles. Next: spring.
- r7: Spring (#a8c46a): a pale fresh dome on the grey trunk -- beech's early leaf. The lighter colour shows the lobes' inking more; acceptable. Next: autumn gold-bronze.
- r8: Autumn: the spec's #c48a2e renders orange-brown, where the third picture's beech is clear golden yellow. Departure from the spec: autumn #d0a032, gold with a bronze edge. Next: winter.
- r9: Winter (#c8b48a, the spec's pale held leaves; drawn as the whole crown until the bare variant, where only the lower limbs keep them): a pale tan dome, reads as marcescent beech. Next: far twins; then a little more trunk under the crown.
- r10: Far twins: the broad low dome holds at 1,045 triangles (full 4,470), the lobes coarser. The full model's trunk is still mostly hidden under the low ring. Next: trunk a little taller and the ring raised, so the grey trunk shows as in the second picture.
- r11: Trunk to 21% of the height, the low ring raised: the short stout grey trunk now shows under a broad low dome, as the second picture -- and the outline (wider than tall, level base on a visible trunk) can no longer be taken for a shrub or the birch's open clumps. Next: autumn again with the new gold.
- r12: Autumn at #d0a032 (the spec's #c48a2e rendered orange-brown): a clear gold dome with a bronze shade on its lower side, on the grey trunk -- the third picture's beech. Keep this departure. Next: the cost count.
- r13: Cost count: 4,470 each, far 1,045. Summer holds: broad low dome on the visible grey trunk.
- r14: Far round again: holds.
- r15: Far round: holds.
- r16: Summer, final: a short stout smooth grey trunk under a broad low dome, wider than tall, its level base on seven long limbs and the dome of overlapping clumps lit as one mass -- the second picture's form, the third's in autumn gold (#d0a032, departure from the spec's #c48a2e). 4,470 triangles, far 1,045. Done.
