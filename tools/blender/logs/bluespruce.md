# bluespruce: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_mountain.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_mountain/bluespruce-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a dense symmetrical cone of drooping tiers to the ground, silver-blue. Close to the second picture's open-grown tree. The tiers' edges are smooth plates where the real tree's are bristly. 1,450 triangles. Next: more, smaller tiers, more raggedness.
- r2: Thirteen smaller ragged tiers: the texture is better but it came out too narrow -- a Christmas tree -- where the pictures are a broad cone about 0.6 as wide as tall. Next: width 10 m for 18.
- r3: Width 10.5 m for 18: now the broad blue cone of the second picture, ragged tier edges. Next: hold; spring's silver tips.
- r4: Held; the broad cone holds against the second picture. Next: spring.
- r5: Held; no change this round.
- r6: Held this round; the broad blue cone matches the second picture. Next: spring's silver new tips.
- r7: Spring: the new tips paler, silvery over the blue-grey; 2,050 triangles. Reads as the spec's silver tips. Next: the far twin.
- r8: Autumn and the far twins: the broad cone and its ragged tiers survive at half the triangles. Next: winter.
- r9: Summer: blue-grey, the broad cone of the second picture. Next: winter.
- r10: Winter: blue-grey, as the spec. Next: lighter upper tiers, the new growth.
- r11: Held this round. Next: lighter upper tiers, the season's new growth showing from the top.
- r12: The upper tiers lighter: the new growth shows from the top, like the second picture's silvery crown. Next: the far twin.
- r13: The far twins with the lighter tops: the cone and its tiers survive; the lighter new growth shows on the twin too. Next: spring.
- r14: Spring: silver new growth from the top down over blue-grey; matches the spec's silver tips and the second picture's crown. Next: summer, final.
- r15: Summer, final: a broad silver-blue cone to the ground, lighter new growth at the top, as the second picture; 1,750-2,050 triangles. Done.
