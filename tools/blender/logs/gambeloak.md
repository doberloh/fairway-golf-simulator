One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_westmtn/gambeloak-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: separate lollipop clumps on bare sticks, a toy; the pictures show a dense rounded oak. Next: clumps a fifth bigger, three more down each stem so the stems hide and the mound closes.
- r2: Bigger clumps down the stems: now a dense rounded oak mound, stems hidden; a little ball-like. Watch: dips in the outline later.
- r3: Holds: a dense rounded oak mound on a clone of grey stems.
- r4: Holds; the edge suckers float like loose leaves. Next: lower, wider suckers sitting on the ground.
- r5: Spring: pale #9db458 new leaves; suckers now sit on the ground. Holds.
- r6: Unchanged; holds.
- r7: Autumn: rust-orange #b0562e over the whole mound -- reads as the oak brush of a Colorado hillside in October. Holds.
- r8: Holds.
- r9: Holds.
- r10: Winter: brown held leaves #8a6440, as the spec. Holds.
- r11: Unchanged; holds.
- r12: Still a round ball a little taller than wide; pictures 1 and 3 and the clonal habit want a broad lumpy mound wider than tall. Next: 5 m by 7.5 m, eight stems, uneven heights.
- r13: Wider now, but at full size it is a blob of big faceted lumps to the ground with every stem hidden -- any bush, not an oak. Pictures 1 and 3: grey stems under an irregular crown of many lobed clumps with notches. Next: five smaller clumps a stem, none below 40% of the height, stems thicker, three small suckers at the edge.
- r14: Now a clonal stand: grey stems under lobed notched clumps, unlike any ball -- but the crowns sit too high, a row of mushrooms, where the pictures' crowns come down near the ground. Next: crown floor at 30% of the height, clumps hung lower and a little bigger.
- r15: Crown floor at 30%: a broad clonal grove of small oaks, grey stems under notched lobed crowns, wider than tall -- reads as Gambel oak brush, not a ball. 2,870 triangles. Holds.
- r16: No far twin (under 15 m). Holds.
- r17: Final, summer: a broad clonal grove of small oaks, grey stems under notched lobed crowns, wider than tall. 2,870 triangles.
