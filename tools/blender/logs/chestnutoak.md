# chestnutoak: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_appalachia.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_appalachia/chestnutoak-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: No pictures gathered; built from the spec and the species. First version: thin trunk under a few flat plates on top -- a bonsai, not a 22 m ridge oak. Next: thicker ridged trunk, four leaders with more side limbs, each end a mass of several lumps, crown from 0.4 of the height, snag visible.
- r2: From the spec (no pictures). Thicker trunk and masses of balls: now a tree, but a broad dome like any oak, and the snag is lost. Next: more upright leaders, side masses alternating high and low with a gap, a longer, thicker snag out of the top -- irregular and upright.
- r3: From the spec. Upright leaders, masses alternating low and high, and the snag shows on _2: an irregular open upright crown, apart from the broad domed oaks. Next: one more mass high on _1, which leans lopsided.
- r4: The middle mass makes _1 upright rather than lopsided; both read as irregular, open, upright oaks with separate masses, _2 with its snag. Holds; next season rounds.
- r5: Spring light green #9ab55a over the dark ridged trunk: reads. Holds.
- r6: Autumn orange-brown #9a6a2e: the muted oak brown, not a maple; reads. Holds.
- r7: Far twins (2,500 / 1,950) keep the upright irregular masses and the snag at half the cost; full trees 5,180 and 3,990. Next (cost): leaders six-sided, outer limbs cheap twigs, to bring _1 under 5,000.
- r8: Cost round: _1 4,850, _2 3,740 (far 2,210 / 1,730). The changed random stream made _1 denser and rounder; _2 still the irregular upright with its snag. Next: _1 keep an eye on it -- open its crown if it stays a dome.
- r9: Winter: drawn in leaf, colour only. _1 has gone a dense round dome (the r4 middle mass on a four-leader tree). Next: the middle mass only on the three-leader tree, so _1 opens again.
- r10: Dropping the middle mass on _1 changed little: its side masses are big and overlap into a dome. _2 holds (irregular, snag). Next: smaller looser side masses, one long reaching limb a leader, so _1 shows sky between masses.
- r11: Smaller looser masses and one long reaching limb a leader: _1 now open and irregular with sky between masses, crooked limbs showing, the snag on top; _2 lopsided upright with its snag. Both read as a rugged ridge oak, apart from the domed oaks and the post oak's cauliflower. Holds.
- r12: Holds.
- r13: Spring light green on the opened crowns: holds.
- r14: Spring: holds.
- r15: 4,660 / 3,740 triangles (far 2,150 / 1,730). Holds.
