# africansumac: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_resort.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_resort/africansumac-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a blocky flat-faced mass with hanging lumps floating free. The pictures are fine and airy, in two or three rounded lobes on forked stems. Next: many small tufts in lobes, no fringe.
- r2: Lobes on forked stems, right, but far too sparse: separate flat leaves of green with sky everywhere. Next: a dark body in each lobe and half again as many, smaller tufts.
- r3: The silhouette is close: a broad crown on forked stems, a dark body under lighter tufts. Too flat on top and darker than the pictures. Next: taller rounder lobes, a lighter green.
- r4: Round lobes and the lighter green hold beside pictures 2 and 3; 4,800 triangles. Holds.
- r5: Unchanged: holds.
- r6: Unchanged: holds beside pictures 2 and 3.
- r7: Spring: lighter new leaf #6a8a4a; holds.
- r8: Autumn: evergreen, unchanged; holds.
- r9: Winter: a shade darker; holds.
- r10: Far twin 2,111 triangles, holds. Close to, a tuft flies free of the crown and the trunk is thin beside the pictures. Next: lobes held closer, a stouter trunk.
- r11: No flying tuft now, but the trunk went stumpy and the crown sits on it with no limbs seen. Next: the trunk back a little, the crown lifted clear of the forks.
- r12: The fork now shows under a lifted crown, as pictures 2 and 3. The crown underside is a hard flat line with bare dome. Next: tufts run lower round the lobes.
- r13: Tufts run round the lobes now, no hard flat underside; the fork still shows. About 5,300 triangles. Holds beside pictures 2 and 3.
- r14: Far twins keep the broad crown and the fork. One tuft stands off the far twin of variant 1, at distance only; left.
- r15: Winter: evergreen, a shade cooler; holds.
- r16: Final, in summer colours: the model the game takes.
