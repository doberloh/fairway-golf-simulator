# hala: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_island.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_island/hala-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: lineup (round-0 script): not a ball -- a forked candelabra on stilt roots -- but the tufts are small upright brushes, the crown narrow and the stilts few; the reference is a broad drooping mop-headed crown of many arching strap-leaf tufts over a cone of prop roots, often leaning. Next: leaves that arch out and hang below the tip, more and wider stilts, a lean.
- r2: silhouette 1: tufts now spiky mops with leaves hanging below the branch tips, a cone of 8-10 stilts, the trunk leaning -- reads as hala/pandanus, unlike any palm or broadleaf. But the leaves are pale and thin, the crown too narrow (mops crowd the middle), and Hala_1/_3 are 81/112 tris over. Next: leaves painted darker, branches spread wider, 18 leaves a tuft, 7-9 stilts.
- r3: silhouette 2: wider candelabra, darker green, each arm ending in a spiky drooping mop over the stilt cone -- reads as hala; all three under their old counts now (1,979 / 1,646 / 1,997). But the mops are thin and see-through where the reference's are dense heavy heads. Next: wider straps (0.08 of their length) and longer (0.34 H), at no cost.
- r4: silhouette 3: wider straps make heavy spiky mop heads that droop past the tips -- reads as pandanus at thumbnail size. The stilt cone is low and the trunk above it slim; the reference's prop roots climb a third of the way up a stout trunk. Next: trunk starts higher on taller stilts (from 0.2-0.32 H), trunk a little thicker.
- r5: silhouette 4: stilt roots now climb to a third of the height under a stouter, leaning trunk; candelabra arms to heavy drooping mops -- the hala outline, distinct from the palms (one crown of fronds) and the banyan (root columns under a dome). Crown reads flat in colour, one green. Next: leaves of the inner mops a shade darker than the outer, for depth.
- r6: silhouette 5: inner mops a shade darker (0.82 at the axis), a little depth in the head; outline holds -- leaning stout trunk on a tall stilt cone, candelabra arms, heavy drooping spiky mops. Next: a final summer render and count.
- r7: silhouette 6, final summer: holds -- reads as hala by outline alone. Hala_1 1,979 tris (was 2,072), Hala_2 1,646 (1,775), Hala_3 1,997 (2,019). The pack has no far twins (strap leaves are flat faces, a twin would be the same model), so no far round.
