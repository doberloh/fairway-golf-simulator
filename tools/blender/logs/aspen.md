# aspen: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_broadleaf.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_broadleaf/aspen-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: Lineup (4,400 tris): a lollipop -- one rounded ball on top of a white stick. Pictures: a narrow open crown of small clumps along the top of the stem, sky between. Next: seven small paired clumps on short alternate branches up the top 40%, a tip clump.
- r2: No longer a lollipop, but seven small clumps read as peas on a stick -- too sparse and too small against the pictures' narrow full crown. Next: ten clumps, a fifth bigger, crown down to 0.52 H.
- r3: Reads now: a slim white pole with a narrow lumpy column of clumps in its top half, like the grove trees in the pictures. Too regular a column; next: every third clump on a longer branch so the outline is ragged.
- r4: Reads as an aspen: a long white pole and a narrow ragged column of round clumps in the top half, a clump jutting on a longer branch. Holds.
- r5: Holds: white pole, narrow ragged column of clumps high up.
- r6: Holds; cost: far twin 977 against 971 shipped. Next: the clump branches as two-segment twigs (16 triangles each).
- r7: Holds after the cost cut (3,515, far 717; shipped 4,370 and 971): a long white pole, a narrow open ragged column of round clumps in its top half, branches showing.
- r8: Autumn: gold clumps on the white pole, the grove pictures' look; holds.
- r9: Far twins (717, shipped 971): pole and narrow open column hold. Holds.
- r10: Final, summer: a long slim white pole, a narrow open ragged column of small round clumps in its top half, sky between -- apart from the birch's splayed stems.
