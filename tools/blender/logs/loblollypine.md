# loblollypine: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_southpines.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_southpines/loblollypine-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First version: crown in the top third on a straight trunk (right), but small, narrow and made of separate cubes; the pictures show a full, wide oval of needles. Next: wider crown, bigger overlapping spiky brushes, more of them.
- r2: Fuller and wider -- an oval in the top third, the right mass against the second and third pictures -- but the faceted balls again (the brush bug). Next: the stellated brush.
- r3: Spiky stars in an oval in the top third -- the outline is right, but it is far too sparse; the pictures are a full, dense mass. Next: clumps of two fat brushes with short spikes, filling the oval.
- r4: Clumps fill it a little, but it is still a sparse star-chart where the pictures show a dense oval. Next: bigger brushes, and four brushes up the stem through the crown to close its middle.
- r5: Now a dense oval of brushes in the top third on a long clean trunk: reads as the second picture's loblolly and nothing like the open longleaf. 5,500-6,000 triangles, over budget; _2 is not visibly flat-topped. Next: 15 limbs and 3 stem brushes for cost; _2's upper limbs level so its top flattens.
- r6: The cut cost kept the dense oval; _2's top is a little flatter. Beside the wider longleaf it needs to be broader still to stay the full, solid one. Next: 12.5 and 11 m across.
- r7: At 12.5 and 11 m: a broad, dense, closed oval (_1) and a flatter broad dome (_2) high on a long clean trunk -- the second and third pictures' loblolly, unlike the open longleaf beside it. Holds.
- r8: Holds: dense broad oval high on a clean trunk. Next: spring's yellow pollen.
- r9: Spring: lighter #6e8f3e, yellow pollen on the cone lumps under the brushes -- small, a hint. Holds.
- r10: Autumn: the cone lumps take the spec's rust 'needle drop' colour -- a hint, the crown stays green. Holds.
- r11: Winter: #567a36; the dense broad oval holds. Next: the far twins.
- r12: Far twins keep the dense oval, but blockier and a little bigger than the models (the fat far brush). Next: the same fix as the longleaf.
- r13: Far twins now the model's oval at its weight, a little faceted, fine at their range; 4,500 (_1) and 5,000 (_2) triangles, twins 1,950 and 2,100. Holds.
- r14: Summer: the broad, dense, closed oval (_1) and flatter dome (_2) high on a clean trunk -- the one solid crown of the three. Holds.
- r15: Summer, final: a straight clean trunk to 60%, a broad dense closed oval of spiky brushes (_2 flatter-topped); 4,520 and 5,020 triangles, far twins 1,940 and 2,110. Done.
