# bougainvillea: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_keys.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_keys/bougainvillea-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: r1: a green ball studded with magenta hexagons -- polka dots. The pictures: arching canes spilling over, magenta masses covering the top and pouring down the sides, little green. Next: a fountain of arching canes, bract clusters along their outer halves so the outline droops at the edges, the green only underneath.
- r2: Fountain of canes: the clusters are faceted magenta hexagons floating in air on the cane ends -- tiles, not spilling bracts. Next: round lobes, fewer and bigger, overlapping into one magenta mass that pours down to the ground, a bigger green mound under them.
- r3: Round bract lobes: big magenta balls sit on the ground round a green ball -- pom-poms, not spilling. Next: six canes, four smaller clusters each along the whole arch, overlapping into magenta ropes that rise from the centre and pour down the sides.
- r4: Magenta ropes: the top is a magenta mass over a green mound -- a flowering shrub at last -- but the bare cane tips stick out at the bottom like legs, and it is still a ball, not spilling. Next: five canes, clusters to the very tips, tapering from big to small so each cane is a drooping tongue of bracts.
- r5: Clusters to the tips: the tips now sit on the ground as magenta feet under a magenta-and-green ball. The fountain idea fails at this size. Next: a mound -- a green core, magenta lobes over its upper skin, and magenta tongues hanging from its rim down the sides, so the mass itself spills.
- r6: Mound with hanging tongues: a magenta jellyfish -- solid magenta cap, tongues hanging like legs, stems showing below, and no green at all where the pictures are half green. Next: mound lower and wider, every third mass green, the tongues shorter and joined to the body.
- r7: Green masses mixed in, tongues joined: a magenta-and-green mound -- but it stands on its stems and the tongues hang as separate balloons, legs under a body. Next: the mound lowered so the tongues reach the ground and hide the stems, the outline a heap with a scalloped, drooping skirt.
- r8: Autumn orange-red (#d05a3a): lower now, no legs, but a heap of smooth balloons -- the bracts in the pictures are papery and ragged. Next: 70 ragged little star sprays over the skin and spilling down the sides, smaller tongues.
- r9: Ragged star sprays over the skin: the outline is ragged and papery now, magenta with green showing, sprays spilling down the sides -- reads as a bougainvillea mound. The low tongues are still smooth balloons. Next: hold and check the cost.
- r10: Holds: a ragged papery magenta mound with green showing. 3,900 triangles, over a shrub's 3,500. Next (cost): four green core lobes, ten upper masses, five tongues.
- r11: Fewer masses: 3,340 triangles, inside budget; the ragged magenta mound with green showing holds.
- r12: Far round (no twin): holds.
- r13: Winter: duller magenta (#a8487e, PLACED for 'fewer bracts', the mesh cannot lose them) on #5a8a3e leaf: holds, the ragged mound.
- r14: Holds: ragged magenta mound, green showing, sprays spilling down.
- r15: Summer, final: a mound to the ground of magenta masses with green showing, seventy ragged papery bract sprays roughening the outline and spilling down the sides. 3,340 triangles.
