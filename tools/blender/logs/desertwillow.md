# desertwillow: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_deserts.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_deserts/desertwillow-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: three stems with hanging leaf lumps. The lumps read as paddle leaves; the pictures show a rounded irregular crown of fine drooping foliage with pink flowers. Next: crowns of small loose drooping clusters, more ends.
- r2: Clusters of wisps: still reads as sticks carrying paddles; the pictures show a full rounded irregular crown of fine foliage. Next: crown masses of small jittery clumps hung from the branch ends.
- r3: Bodies on the ends: still flat paddle-like lumps; the pictures show a full rounded irregular crown. Next: round jittery clumps on every end, wisps hanging out of them.
- r4: Round bodies on the ends: still read as big single leaves on sticks; the pictures show an irregular crown of fine foliage clusters. Next: each end three small lumps in a loose cluster, wisps hanging, sky between clusters.
- r5: Three-lump clusters on each end: better, an irregular crown of separate clusters, but still thin beside the pictures' full crown. Next: four bigger lumps a cluster, two hanging wisps.
- r6: Spring: four-lump clusters read thicker, but every cluster sits at one height -- a flat umbrella of separate blobs; the pictures' crown is rounded and irregular. Next: the middle branch of each stem climbs, the outer ones reach out lower; lumps shaded rounder (normals less bent).
- r7: Climbing middle branches, rounder lumps: now a rounded irregular crown of clusters with sky between on three leaning stems, like the pictures; the pink flowers hide inside the clusters. Next: flowers on every end, set out on the cluster tops.
- r8: Autumn: yellow #b0a050 crown, pods (accent) set out over the clusters; the rounded irregular crown on leaning stems holds.
- r9: Winter (deciduous, drawn in leaf until its bare variant): pods as accent; holds.
- r10: Cost count: 5,470 triangles, over budget for a 6 m tree (the cluster lumps at 10 x 6). Next: lumps back to 8 x 5, two flowers an end.
- r11: Cluster lumps 8 x 5, two flowers an end: 3,850 triangles (was 5,470), crown unchanged in outline; holds.
- r12: Holds: irregular open crown of clusters on leaning stems.
- r13: Holds.
- r14: Holds.
- r15: Final: three leaning crooked stems under an open irregular crown of separate leafy clusters with wisps hanging and pink flowers on top, sky between. 3,850 triangles.
