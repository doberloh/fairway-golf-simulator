# tanoak: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_redwood.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_redwood/tanoak-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: lineup (round-0 script): one ellipsoid cloud on a bare pole -- a lollipop, the ball the owner named. Reference (under redwoods): tall and narrow, a straight leader, short upswept limbs, dense clumps stacked into a tall irregular egg. Next: rebuild from a leader with upswept limbs, a clump pair on each, a cap; narrower (W 11 and 9.5, was 14 and 12).
- r2: silhouette 1: leader and upswept limbs right, but the clumps are small and far apart -- reads as a Japanese cloud-pruned pine, not a dense tanoak. Next: clumps half as big again and limbs shorter, so they overlap into one tall bumpy egg with only a little trunk showing; bigger cap.
- r3: silhouette 2: now a tall narrow egg of stacked clumps on a straight leader, trunk showing below 0.3 H -- no longer a lollipop, and unlike the oaks' broad domes or the conifers' spires. But the clumps are smooth even balls (a stack of fluffy balls), the crown starts too high and the green is the conifers' blue-green where the reference is olive. Next: crown from 0.25 H, second lump of each pair out and down for a ragged side, more jitter, an olive sheet tint; Tanoak_2 six limbs (room under its old 1,991).
- r4: silhouette 3: olive now, a tall ragged column of clumps from 0.25 H with the leader and limbs showing between -- reads as an understory tanoak, distinct from every dome and spire in the game. The lobes look faceted (jitter 0.16 on the 12-sided lobe) and the far twins cost 582 (old 543) because their limbs barely shrink. Next: jitter 0.12; the far twin skips the limbs (hidden in the clumps at that distance), drawn from their own random stream so the layout holds.
- r5: silhouette 4: far twins now 330 tris (old 543) with no limbs; the new limb stream re-laid the near models, still a tall narrow ragged column on a visible leader. The clumps are still round balls stacked up. Next: each main clump flat-bottomed (squash 0.5) and lower (z 0.7), so the column reads as stacked shelves of leaf with shadowed undersides rather than balls.
- r6: silhouette 5: flat-bottomed clumps give shadowed shelves under each, the column reads as stacked leaf masses on upswept limbs rather than balls -- holds as tanoak. The top is a round knob where young forest tanoaks taper. Next: cap taller (1.35 of its width) for a narrower, slightly pointed top.
- r7: silhouette 6, final summer: the taller cap narrows the top a little; holds -- a tall, narrow, ragged column of olive leaf shelves on a straight leader with upswept limbs, trunk bare below a quarter of the height. Tanoak_1 and _2 1,896 tris each (were 1,991). Next: far round.
- r8: far round 1: twins 330 tris (old 543) keep the outline, but their 20-face lobes sit inside the full lobes' outline, so the column looks thinner and gappier at a distance. Next: the twin's lobes 1.15x bigger.
- r9: far round 2: twin lobes 1.15x fill the column out to the full model's outline at 330 tris (old twins 543). Final.
- r13: r20: the owner on trees as balls: rebuilt as it grows, a tapered stem, limbs forking into branches, small separate clusters of leaves with trunk and sky between; the clumps on limbs are gone.
- r14: r21: r20's clusters sat in a few big plates at the branch ends: many small clusters strung along forking branches (grown.py broadleaf).
- r15: r22: fuller: more limbs and clusters, closer to the photographs' dense irregular crown.
- r16: r23: the owner: literally squares for leaves. Clusters of long pointed leaves on the twigs.
- r17: r24: leaves right; crown thin beside the photographs: more clusters.
- r18: r25: far twin cut.
