# scotchbroom: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_pacific.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_pacific/scotchbroom-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: fourteen switches with pale lumps along them. A pale torch; the real broom is a rounded green fountain of fine stems solid with small yellow flowers. Next: thirty finer stems, a broader fountain, smaller yellow lumps in numbers, sheet tinted yellow.
- r2: Fine switches, yellow dots: the fountain reads but the yellow is sparse dots, where the pictures are solid yellow. Next: flowers bigger and denser, a broader rounder shrub.
- r3: Denser, bigger flowers: solid yellow now, but the shape is a fan opening upward where the pictures are a rounded bush. Next: switches arch outward and fall back, heights more even, some green showing low.
- r4: Switch ends on a dome: still a yellow column, because the model is taller than wide; the pictures are wider than tall. Next: 2.0 m high by 2.6 m across, flowers a little smaller.
- r5: Wider than tall: a bush now, but the yellow bunches on one side. Next: forty switches, so flowers spread evenly round it.
- r6: Forty switches: a rounded bush, yellow all round, like the second picture. Next: judge it out of flower (summer is green switches only).
- r7: Spring: solid yellow on green, as the first two pictures. Next: summer, green switches alone.
- r8: Out of flower: a rounded mound of green switches, which is what it is outside May. Next: hold.
- r9: Summer: green switches, a rounded mound. Next: hold.
- r10: Scale: 2.0 by 2.6 m against 1-3 m -- right. Next: fewer flower lumps a switch for cost, keeping the yellow solid.
- r11: Spring: a solid yellow mound, as the second picture; 6,312 faces, of which most are 80-face flower lumps. Next: twenty-face flowers, twenty-eight switches.
- r12: The cost pass: 3,248 faces, from 6,312; out of flower a green mound, as summer is. Next: spring check, the yellow still solid.
- r13: Autumn: the green mound; holds. Next: spring yellow after the pass.
- r14: Winter: green stems, a mound, as the spec. Next: spring yellow after the cost pass.
- r15: Spring, final: a rounded mound solid with yellow, as the second picture; 3,248 faces. Done.
- r18: Second cost pass: 22 switches, a coarse centre -- 4,100 to 2,800 triangles; the yellow mound holds.
- r19: Silhouette check: a rounded mound, as a broom bush is in the pictures (yellow in spring); a shrub, left unchanged.
