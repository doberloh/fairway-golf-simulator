# dogwood: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_piedmont.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_piedmont/dogwood-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First version: low-forked tree with four layers of flat pads; reads as a layered tree but crown sits too high and narrow on a long bare trunk, bracts too small to see. Next: broader flatter dome, layers lower, more pads, bigger bracts.
- r2: Broader dome now, but so many pads the layers merged into one dense green dome and the trunk hid (10,600 triangles). Next: fewer, flatter pads with open gaps between the four layers, lowest layer lifted so the short trunk shows.
- r3: Fewer flatter pads and the trunk shows, but it reads as a flat-bottomed mushroom on a stick, not the layered dome of picture 01. Next: stepped layers with clear gaps, lowest widest and lower, upper ones stepping in to a rounded top; taller tree (7 m).
- r4: Spring: white pads in stepped layers -- the layering finally shows, but as thin grey discs stacked like a pagoda, sparse beside picture 01's full white dome. Next: thicker, more pads, upper layers wider, brighter spring white leaf.
- r5: Spring: thicker pads in four layers make a broad layered dome, the outline right against picture 01; but white on white is flat and greyish, little depth between layers. Next: stronger shading low in the crown so the layers separate.
- r6: Autumn: red-purple layered dome with sky through the middle and the short trunk below -- reads as dogwood, distinct from the Pacific one's three thin shelves. Berries lost red on red (acceptable). Holds.
- r7: Winter (drawn in leaf; bare variant later): colour fine, but the brown winter-bud accent shows as dead patches on the pads. Next: winter accent nearly leaf green.
- r8: Far round (no twin; under 15 m): the layered dome reads; 6,640 triangles, over the tree budget. Next (cost): pads as 80-triangle lowballs, cheaper bracts.
- r9: Cost round: pads as 80-triangle lowballs, 4,550 triangles (was 6,640); the layered dome unchanged. Holds.
- r10: Holds: a low-forked broad dome of level pads in four layers with sky between, against picture 01.
- r11: Holds: layered low dome.
- r12: Holds.
- r13: Holds.
- r14: Holds in summer; spring check next after the cost round's cheaper pads and bracts.
- r15: Spring after the cost round: the layered dome holds, but the white pads read as grey stone slabs -- the crown's dark core and low shade turn white grey. Next: shallower shading (low .58, core .8) so the bloom reads white.
- r16: Spring: shallower shading -- the bloom now reads white, layered pads with the dark branches between, like pictures 01-03. Holds; final summer next.
- r17: Final summer: silhouette a low-forked broad dome of level pads in four layers with sky between -- reads as flowering dogwood, unlike the Pacific dogwood's three thin shelves on a tall stem; summer depth kept with the shallower shading. 4,554 triangles. Done.
