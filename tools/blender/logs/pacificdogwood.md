# pacificdogwood: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_pacific.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_pacific/pacificdogwood-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a small cloud crown, 40 flat cream bracts. Far too few bracts; the real tree is white all over in spring in layered horizontal sprays. Next: tiered flat sprays, bracts in hundreds, white.
- r2: Four tiers of flat sprays: the layered habit reads, but the bracts are too small to see and the tiers too sparse (a pagoda). Next: fuller sprays, bracts twice the size and half as many again.
- r3: Fuller sprays, bigger bracts: white now shows, but it lies on the sprays' tops like snow on a cake; the pictures scatter the bracts over the whole face. Next: bracts tilted out round the sprays' edges as well as on top.
- r4: Bracts scattered over the sprays: they sank inside the leaf lobes and vanished. Next: bracts set on the sprays' upper skin, above the lobes.
- r5: Bracts on the sprays' upper skin: white shows over the whole crown now, as in the third picture. The sides are still bare green. Next: a few bracts on the sprays' rims.
- r6: Bracts on the rims too: white over the whole crown, like the third picture. Next: judge it in spring's white and autumn's red.
- r7: Spring: white bracts over a green crown, as the third picture. Next: autumn's red-pink.
- r8: Autumn red-pink: a red layered tree, as dogwoods turn. Next: summer, out of flower.
- r9: Summer, out of bloom: a small layered tree in plain green, like the dogwood in the second picture's shade. Next: hold.
- r10: Scale: 9 m against 6-15 m -- right. No change this round.
- r11: Counted: 18,665 faces -- the bracts are 80-face spheres, twenty a spray. Next: flat four-petal bracts, three tiers of four sprays.
- r12: The cost pass: 2,873 faces, from 18,665, the bracts flat four-petal crosses -- but the sprays went faceted and thin, plates on a bonsai, where the pictures are a full layered crown. Next: six lobes a spray, bigger, still coarse.
- r13: Six rounder lobes a spray: the crown is full again in autumn red, layered, 3,353 faces. Next: spring's white once more, after the change.
- r14: Winter: should be bare -- owed to the seasons step; the layered form reads. Next: spring's white after round 13's fuller sprays.
- r15: Spring after round 13's fuller sprays: the flat bracts are too small now -- the crown reads green with white flecks where the third picture is white all over. Next: bracts half as big again.
- r16: Bracts half as big again: white over every spray now, close to the second picture; seen from the side, as a golfer sees it, the sprays edges are still plain green where the third picture is white to the rim. Next: a third of the bracts on the sprays rims.
- r17: A third of the bracts on the rims: white to the sprays edges from the side now, as the third picture; 3,353 faces. Done; its bare winter variant is owed to the seasons step.
- r18: Second cost pass: ten bracts a spray, not sixteen -- the bracts were 3,000 of its 5,300 triangles; now 4,100. Still white across the sprays in spring, a little less dense.
- r19: Silhouette lineup: the tiers are there but run together into a fuzzy cone. Next: three thin flat shelves with clear sky between them, wider at the bottom, sprays horizontal, the bracts on top.
- r20: Silhouette: three level shelves with sky between, lowest widest -- reads as tiers now. The lowest shelf breaks into separate pads on sticks. Next: wider sprays so each tier is one lumpy shelf, a small cap on top.
- r21: Silhouette: three level shelves and a small flat cap on the leader, sky between the tiers -- reads as a layered dogwood. Holds; no change.
- r22: Silhouette: level tiers and a cap -- holds.
- r23: Autumn: the red tiers read as layered shelves, distinct from the vine maple's red clumps beside it; holds.
- r24: Spring: the tiers read, but the bracts are buried in the flattened shelves -- a few white specks where the pictures are white all over. Next: the bracts lifted above the lobes' tops, nine a spray instead of seven. 3,306 triangles (round 19: 4,130).
- r25: Spring: the bracts now lie white on top of every shelf, so the tiers read as white layers -- the layered silhouette with its flowers on top, as in the third picture. Still more green than the pictures' white. Next: the bracts a third bigger.
- r26: Far round (no far twin): the bracts a third bigger read as white on every shelf; holds.
- r27: Holds (no far twin).
- r28: Summer, final of the silhouette pass: a small tree of three thin level shelves, sky between, the lowest widest, a small cap -- the layered dogwood outline; in spring its bracts lie white on every shelf. 3,690 triangles (round 19: 4,130). Done.
