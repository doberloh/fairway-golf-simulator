# mesquite: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_desert.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_desert/mesquite-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: lineup (round-0 script): one ellipsoid cloud on a short pale trunk -- the 'big green fluffy ball' the owner named. Reference: low dark crooked trunk forking at once into arching limbs, a broad flat-topped feathery crown wider than tall, drooping at the edge. Next: rebuild from limbs, a shelf clump per tip, tassels at the edge; dark bark and darker leaf in the sheet tints.
- r2: silhouette 1: dark crooked trunk and arching limbs read as mesquite at last, crown wide; but the clumps are faceted flat pads and the top is gappy like a bonsai. Next: shelf clumps on the smooth 144-tri lobe, taller; a three-clump lid over the middle; three tassels a tip.
- r3: silhouette 2: dark arching limbs under a broad crown -- distinct from ironwood and the oaks -- but the crown is still a set of round puffs and taller than the reference's (which is about 1.5x wider than tall). Next: tips spread to 0.75-1.0 of the radius, a lower flatter lid, shelf clumps wider and flatter, tassels longer and hanging lower.
- r4: silhouette 3: wide, about 1.4x wider than tall, dark crooked limbs, hanging tassels at the rim like the reference's drooping fringe -- reads as mesquite. Crown still a set of smooth puffs; Mesquite_2 (four limbs) breaks into separate bits. Next: shelf lobes more ragged (jitter .28) for a feathery edge; Mesquite_2 five limbs.
- r5: silhouette 4: Mesquite_2 with five limbs is now a wide flat shelf, ragged lobes help the feathery edge; all three read as a low wide dark-limbed mesquite, not a ball. The reference is often multi-stemmed from the ground; ours is one trunk. Next: a second, smaller trunk leaning out from the foot on Mesquite_1 and _3.
- r6: silhouette 5: a second leaning stem from the foot on _1 and _3 makes them multi-stemmed like the reference; the crown, a broad flat dark shelf about 1.5x wider than tall with tassels hanging past its rim, reads as mesquite. The tassels hang like long pods. Next: tassels shorter and smaller.
- r7: silhouette 6: shorter tassels now read as a drooping feathery fringe rather than pods; low, wide, flat-topped dark-limbed crown -- holds, reads as mesquite and unlike ironwood, oaks or palo verde. Mesquite_1 4,151 tris (was 4,223), _2 4,045, _3 4,151. Next the far round.
- r8: far round 1: twins match, but cost 1,837 (old twins 1,343). Next: the twin draws one tassel a tip, no second stem; those from their own random streams so the twin keeps the full model's layout.
- r9: far round 2: twins 1,353 each (old 1,343), same broad dark-limbed shelf; the near models re-laid by the new streams but keep the outline -- final: Mesquite_1 4,151, _2 4,045, _3 4,151 (old 4,223).
