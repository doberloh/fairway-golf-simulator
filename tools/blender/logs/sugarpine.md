# sugarpine: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_westmtn.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_westmtn/sugarpine-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a 50 m pole whose arms and pads are so small they vanish at sheet size; the pictures show long level arms with substantial foliage along them. Next: spread 18 m, pads half again bigger, cones doubled (0.8 m) so they read.
- r2: Bigger pads still vanish: six arms at the very top make a pole with a topknot, while the pictures carry foliage down the upper half with the longest arms high. Next: ten arms from half height, the top three the long flags, short branches from 36%.
- r3: Foliage now down the upper half with longer arms at the top, but the flat pads read as thin lily pads and the crown is narrower than the pictures' (width about 0.4-0.5 of height). Next: spread 22 m, thicker pads, a hanging fringe under each arm tip.
- r4: Thicker pads and hanging fringes give the crown body, but now the top is a rounded mass that could pass for the ponderosa; the flags do not stand out. Next: lower arms short (0.3-0.45 of the radius), the top three long (1.0-1.1) so the flags read.
- r5: Spring: the long top arms reach out now, but their foliage is a few big flat pads that read as broad leaves, the arm itself hidden, the cones hidden under them. Next: many small ragged tufts along each arm on alternate sides, hanging a little; cones hung clear below the tips.
- r6: Small ragged tufts along each arm: now a tall pole with level arms of needles, the top three reaching out as flags, apart from the ponderosa's upswept tufts. Still a little narrow beside picture 1, and the cones (0.6 m wide on a 50 m tree) are a few pixels at sheet size. Next: flags 1.05-1.2 of the radius, lower arms drooping at the tips, cones a little bigger.
- r7: Autumn (evergreen, unchanged). Wider flags and drooping lower arm tips: the tall pole with long level arms at the top reads apart from the ponderosa's tufted crown, like picture 1. Holds.
- r8: Far twin keeps the pole and level arms at 2,200 triangles (full 4,900). Holds.
- r9: Holds: tall pole, long level arms at the top.
- r10: Winter #4c704a, cones a darker #8a6a46. Holds.
- r11: Unchanged; holds.
- r12: Holds.

- r16: Cost: 4,570 triangles now (far twin 1,970); the far twin keeps the pole and the long top flags, which still read beside pictures 1 and 3. Holds.
- r17: Final, summer: a very tall pole, short drooping arms down its middle and three long level flags at the top hung with cones -- the sugar pine's outline, apart from the ponderosa. 4,570 triangles.
