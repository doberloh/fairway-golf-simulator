# seagrape: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_keys.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_keys/seagrape-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: r1: a bonsai -- small pads of leaf on long bare sprawling stems. The pictures: a broad dense dome (or hedge) from a few short stems, its edge scalloped by big round leaf plates, grape strings hanging. Next: four short stems under one close dome of broad lobes, round leaf plates standing proud of its skin for the scalloped edge, hanging grape strings outside it.
- r2: One dome on four short stems: a broad tree now, but a generic one -- the leaf plates are lost in the dome and the grapes are specks. Next: bigger plates stood edge-out round the skin so the outline is scalloped by circles, the dome lower and rounder, grape strings longer and rose-pink (summer green-to-pink, picture 3).
- r3: Plates stood edge-out: the dome's top bristles with thin vertical slivers -- a ragged hedge, not round leaves. Pink grape strings now show below the edge (good). Next: plates turned every way (mostly facing out and up), so a mix of circles and ellipses scallops the outline.
- r4: Plates turned every way: the dome's outline is scalloped by round leaves now, on short stems, pink strings under it -- a sea grape at a glance, though dark and solid. Next: 60 plates, a shade lighter than the dome, so the round leaves read on its face too.
- r5: 60 lighter plates: the round leaves now read on the dome's face and edge, pink strings below, short stems -- a sea grape. Holds; next a colour check.
- r6: Holds: the scalloped dome of round plates on short stems. Next: spring colours (new leaves, unripe green grapes).
- r7: Spring: fresher green, the grape strings unripe green (#9aae6a) -- holds, the scalloped dome on short stems.
- r8: Autumn at the spec's #8a5a3a: the whole dome turns rust -- the spec means 'some red leaves', and the game paints the leaf role whole. PLACED: autumn leaf #74703a, an olive with a red cast, so it stays a green shrub with a turn in it.
- r9: Summer: holds -- the scalloped dome of round leaf plates on short stems, rose grape strings under its edge.
- r10: Holds: the scalloped dome. 2,470 triangles, inside a shrub's budget.
- r11: Holds. No far twin (a 2,470-triangle shrub).
- r12: Far round (no twin): holds -- scalloped dome, round plates readable.
- r13: Winter (#56843c): holds, grape strings kept (the spec names none for winter, the mesh cannot drop them; the game may hide the accent).
- r14: Holds: scalloped dome of round plates, short stems, grape strings.
- r15: Summer, final: a broad dome on four short stems, its outline scalloped by sixty round leaf plates, rose-pink grape strings hanging under the edge -- reads as sea grape. 2,470 triangles.
