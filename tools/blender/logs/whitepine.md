# whitepine: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_newengland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_newengland/whitepine-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: trunk with separate flat shelves; _1 swept to the lee reads at once as an old flag pine (silhouette: shelves one side, nothing else in the game looks so). But the pads are big flat plates -- bonsai -- where the three pictures show many tufted irregular tiers. Next: eight tiers, pads smaller and thicker (0.65 of width), more pads a tier.
- r2: Eight tiers of smaller thicker pads: a thin stick of round pads -- bonsai bamboo, narrower and sparser than all three pictures, whose crowns are broad irregular cones of distinct ragged shelves. 6,000 triangles. Next: each shelf a flat ragged star of tufts (ct.skirt laid off-centre on the limb, cheap) instead of round lumps, crown wider and lower, seven tiers.
- r3: Shelves as flat ragged stars of tufts: now the outline is white pine's -- separated level shelves, _1 flagged to the lee like the first and third pictures' old trees; no other tree in the game has it. The shelves are thinner and sparser than the pictures' broad tiers. 3,800 / 4,600 triangles. Next: four limbs on the lower tiers, shelves a fifth wider.
- r4: Four limbs on the low tiers, wider shelves: _1 is a full flagged crown of ragged level shelves, close to the first and third pictures; _2 a broad tiered cone. The lowest shelves of _2 render near-black (the crown shade's centre sits too high). Next: shade centre lower and deeper.
- r5: Shade centre lowered: _2's black bottom shelf is gone, both read as tiered white pines with ragged level shelves, _1 flagged. Close to the first and third pictures. Next: the cost (5,100 triangles on _2).
- r6: Cost: limbs four-sided, trunk seven: 4,400 / 4,900 triangles, looks the same -- ragged level shelves, _1 flagged. Far twins are 2,700-2,900, over half: next cost pass. Next: spring.
- r7: Spring (#5f8a5a): barely moves from summer, as an evergreen should; the shelves still read. Holds. Next: autumn, and the far twins' cost (inner pads dropped at distance).
- r8: Autumn (#58825a, the spec's yellow inner needles not drawn -- invisible at play distance): unchanged evergreen shelves. The far twin now keeps only the outer pad a limb, 2,100 triangles from 2,900, the pads on their own stream so the far outline matches the full one. Next: winter, then the far round.
- r9: Winter (#557e58): unchanged colour. The pads' own stream moved _1's shelves: still level ragged shelves swept to the lee, the old flag pine. Holds. Next: the far twins.
- r10: Far twins: outer pads only, 2,090 triangles against 4,480 / 4,870 -- the level ragged shelves and _1's flag hold, a little sparser, right at distance. Holds.
- r11: Summer, unchanged: holds -- level ragged shelves, _1 the flagged old pine.
- r12: Autumn again: unchanged evergreen shelves; holds.
- r13: Cost count: _1 4,480, _2 4,870; far twins 2,090, of which 990 is bark -- the limbs barely shrink at distance. Next: the far twin drops its limbs (hidden under the shelves at that range), each limb on its own random stream so the full model and twin keep the same shelves.

The most recognisable outline in the pack. 4,390 / 4,960 triangles, far 1,740 / 2,030 (pads kept, limbs dropped). Done.
