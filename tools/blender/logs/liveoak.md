# liveoak: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_southoaks.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_southoaks/liveoak-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: first version: wide low outline and the dipping limbs are right, but the canopy reads as separate bonsai pads with sky between and the trunk is thin and hidden; the pictures are one dense broad mass with a dark cave under it. Next: bigger overlapping clumps, a second middle ring, a fatter trunk.
- r2: denser broad mushroom now, wider than tall, limbs showing under it; but the squashed clumps read as stacked pancakes with hard disc edges, not one dense canopy. Next: rounder clumps (squash .78), bigger and more varied sizes so they merge.
- r3: rounder clumps merge into one broad dense dome, wider than tall: reads as a live oak's mushroom, not the bur oak's flat ragged plate. But the rim is one even row of balls at one height, hiding the trunk. Next: rim heights varied (low over the dipping limbs, high between) so the lower edge is irregular and the trunk shows.
- r4: rim heights now vary, so the lower edge is irregular: a broad dense dome on a short massive trunk with limbs showing under it, wider than tall -- reads as a live oak and apart from the bur oak's flat plate and the generic oak's few heavy masses. Holds; next look at the trunk showing.

- r6: holds: broad dense low dome, irregular rim, short trunk and sinuous limbs under it. Next: spring colours.
