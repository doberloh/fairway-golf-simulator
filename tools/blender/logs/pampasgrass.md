# pampasgrass: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_resort.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_resort/pampasgrass-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a thin tuft of wiry leaves and a few fat plumes. The pictures are a big dense mound of long fine leaves under a fan of many thin plumes. Next: a mound with a core, 24 thin plumes.
- r2: Fan of thin plumes reads now. The mound is a hard dark faceted pot with straight wire leaves. Next: a softer lower core, leaves in four curved segments falling to the ground, more plumes.
- r3: Plumes good. The mound is a dark pot with wires coming out. Next: a broad dome, leaves starting over its surface and running down to the ground.
- r4: The dome and leaves now read as the second picture. Too even in colour and too few plumes. Next: leaves darker toward their tips over a shaded dome, a third more plumes.
- r5: Too far: dark tips lost every leaf and it is one flat green hump; plumes merge into a single mass. Next: leaves lighter at the tips over a darker dome, fewer thinner plumes.
- r6: Leaves light at the tips over a darker dome, thinner plumes with gaps between: reads as the first two pictures. Holds.
- r7: Spring: last year plumes gone tan #b8b088 over fresh leaves; holds.
- r8: Autumn: full cream plumes over greyer leaves, the season it is at its best; holds.
- r9: Winter: straw-tinged leaves, plumes faded; stays standing, as the pictures; holds.
- r10: No far twin: it is seen close, and 3,600 triangles, planted thinly. Holds.
- r11: Reads well from the side; the dome still ends in a hard edge on the ground. Next: a skirt of shorter leaves over its foot.
- r12: The skirt softens the foot. The plumes stand in a tight fan at the top where the pictures spread them across the whole mound. Next: wider fan, outer ones lower, plumes fuller.
- r13: The fan spreads across the mound and the outer plumes lean out low, as pictures 1 and 3. Holds.
- r14: No far twin: a small plant, gone from view by the distance a twin would help. The fan holds.
- r15: Winter: the mound went olive-brown, which breaks the owner rule that winter stays green here. Next: the winter leaf set to the autumn leaf.
- r16: Final, in summer colours: the model the game takes.
