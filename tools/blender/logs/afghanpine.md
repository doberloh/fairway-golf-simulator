# afghanpine: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_resort.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_resort/afghanpine-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: whorled flat tiers like torn paper, sky all through it, narrow cone. The pictures show a full egg of layered needle masses, dense almost to the ground. Next: an egg of many small ragged masses round a dark core.
- r2: Egg of masses round a core: the right idea, but narrow, and the masses read as flat discs with too even an edge. Next: wider low down, more smaller masses, each with a drooping bough tip.
- r3: Egg shape better, but a hard dark core with thin flat shelves sticking out, and 6,000 triangles. Next: a smaller core, thicker puffs over it, no shelves.
- r4: Thick puffs and no shelves: now a dense tree of layered masses like the first picture, though chunky and a little narrow. Next: half as many again, smaller, the egg wider low down.
- r5: Full layered egg, dense to low on the trunk, wide at the bottom, as pictures 1 and 3; 5,000 triangles. Holds.
- r6: Unchanged: holds beside pictures 1 and 3.
- r7: Spring: fresher tips #47703f; holds.
- r8: Autumn: evergreen, unchanged; holds.
- r9: Winter: a shade darker; holds.
- r10: Far twin 2,283 triangles, the same masses, holds beside the model. Beside the pictures every tree stands dead straight. Next: a slight lean, carried up the crown.
- r11: A slight lean through the crown: a row will not be posts. Holds beside pictures 1 and 3.
- r12: Holds: full egg, layered edge, trunk seen only near the ground.
- r13: Holds, unchanged: full egg, dark needle masses, trunk seen near the ground only.
- r14: Far twins keep the egg and the layered edge; nothing lost at distance.
- r15: Winter: an evergreen, a shade cooler; holds.
- r16: Final, in summer colours: the model the game takes.
