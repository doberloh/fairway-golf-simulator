# garryoak: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_pacific.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_pacific/garryoak-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: crooked limbs ending in flat clumps. Reads as an acacia on thin legs; the real oak is a broad dense rounded crown, a heavy short trunk, the crown reaching low. Next: thicker trunk, deeper overlapping clumps, crown lower and fuller.
- r2: One dense crown over heavy limbs: now a mushroom lid, smooth, no limbs showing, too regular. Real crowns are broad and lumpy with gaps where the crooked limbs show. Next: the central mass smaller and higher, more and bigger limb clumps of uneven size, wider than tall.
- r3: Smaller high centre, uneven limb clumps: lumpy and broad, close to the second picture. Still a touch narrow for an open-grown oak. Next: spread 15% wider, the top a little flatter.
- r4: Widened 15%: broad and lumpy like the second picture. The trunk is a stub under the crown. Next: the fork a little higher so the trunk and the first limbs show.
- r5: Higher fork: the trunk and first limbs show under a broad lumpy crown, as in the second picture. Next: hold the form; vary the two variants' crowns more.
- r6: Second variant with six limbs: the two now differ, both broad and lumpy over a visible trunk. Next: judge the colours by season (spring's pale new leaves, autumn's dull brown).
- r7: Spring's pale new leaves: right against the second picture's spring green; form holds. Next: autumn.
- r8: Autumn's dull brown-yellow: reads as a fading oak; uniform but the real tree is too. Next: the far twin.
- r9: Far twins: both keep the broad lumpy outline in a fifth of the faces. Next: summer, final proportions.
- r10: Scale: 15 and 12 m against the spec's 10-20 m and the 1.8 m post -- right. No change this round.
- r11: Spring again, unchanged; counted: 4,905 faces, about 8,000 triangles, over budget. Next: five bigger lobes a clump.
- r12: The cost pass: five bigger lobes a clump, a coreless centre -- 3,229 faces, from 4,905; still broad and lumpy over a visible trunk. Next: autumn check.
- r13: Autumn after the cost pass: dull brown-yellow, broad and lumpy; holds. Next: the far twin.
- r14: Winter, far twins: the twins hold the broad lumpy outline. Winter should be bare grey limbs with moss -- the bare variant is owed to the seasons step (PLAN 3), not this pack. Next: spring.
- r15: Spring, final: pale new leaves on a broad lumpy crown over a short heavy trunk, as the second picture; 3,229-3,721 faces. Done; its bare winter variant is owed to the seasons step.
- r18: Second cost pass: four lobes a limb clump, six in the centre -- 5,700 to 4,700 triangles. Still broad and lumpy over a visible trunk.
