- r2: One dense crown over heavy limbs: now a mushroom lid, smooth, no limbs showing, too regular. Real crowns are broad and lumpy with gaps where the crooked limbs show. Next: the central mass smaller and higher, more and bigger limb clumps of uneven size, wider than tall.
- r3: Smaller high centre, uneven limb clumps: lumpy and broad, close to the second picture. Still a touch narrow for an open-grown oak. Next: spread 15% wider, the top a little flatter.
- r4: Widened 15%: broad and lumpy like the second picture. The trunk is a stub under the crown. Next: the fork a little higher so the trunk and the first limbs show.
- r5: Higher fork: the trunk and first limbs show under a broad lumpy crown, as in the second picture. Next: hold the form; vary the two variants' crowns more.
- r6: Second variant with six limbs: the two now differ, both broad and lumpy over a visible trunk. Next: judge the colours by season (spring's pale new leaves, autumn's dull brown).

- r8: Autumn's dull brown-yellow: reads as a fading oak; uniform but the real tree is too. Next: the far twin.
- r9: Far twins: both keep the broad lumpy outline in a fifth of the faces. Next: summer, final proportions.
