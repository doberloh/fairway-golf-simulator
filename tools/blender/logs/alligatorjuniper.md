# alligatorjuniper: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_deserts.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_deserts/alligatorjuniper-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: checkered trunk, limbs, flat pads at the ends. Reads as a bonsai of flat plates; the pictures show a broad dense irregular crown of tufted masses. Next: more, rounder masses filling the crown, gaps but not plates.
- r2: More clumps: still flat umbrella pads on stilts; the pictures show a dense irregular crown with masses through its depth. Next: clumps in the crown volume, not only at limb ends.
- r3: Masses on the limbs' lower halves: still a canopy on a tall bare stem; the pictures show the crown coming down low on a short massive trunk. Next: trunk shorter, crown centre lower.
- r4: Shorter trunk, lower crown: better, but still a regular broccoli; the pictures are lopsided and rugged. Next: limb reach varied 0.5-0.95 of the radius for a lopsided crown, masses hung lower on the limbs.
- r5: Varied reach and lower masses: now lopsided, the crown coming lower; reads closer to picture 1. _1 is 5,250 triangles, a little over. Next: check in the season rounds, trim if needed.
- r6: Spring: blue-green #6d8a78, the colour reads apart from the green trees; holds in shape.
- r7: Holds as a lopsided blue-green crown on a short massive checkered trunk; _2 a little regular. Next: a silver dead snag on _2 too (picture 2's gaunt look).
- r8: Row scale: the masses are smooth round balls and read as a broadleaf; the pictures' crown is many small ragged tufts. Next: each mass three small ragged lumps (high jitter), not one smooth clump.
- r9: Ragged three-lump tufts: the crown now reads as a juniper's coarse ragged blue-green masses with crooked limbs between, close to pictures 1-2; _2 a little thin. Next: tufts a little bigger.
- r10: Bigger ragged tufts: fuller, still a juniper's coarse masses with limbs between; 4,690 and 3,680 triangles. Holds.
- r11: Cost round: 4,690 / 3,680; holds.
- r12: Holds.
- r13: Holds; shares the tuft helper, so its masses get the same less-bent normals next round (puffier texture).
- r14: Puffier tufts (normals bent less): coarse ragged blue-green masses, lopsided, on a massive checkered trunk; reads as a juniper, apart from the brighter green Aleppo pine. Holds.
- r15: Final: a massive checkered short trunk, heavy crooked limbs, lopsided coarse ragged blue-green masses, a silver dead snag. 4,690 / 3,680 triangles.
