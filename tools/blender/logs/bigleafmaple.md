- r3: Rounder top, less flare: matches the third picture's form. The outline is a little too smooth. Next: three lobes standing proud of the crown.
- r4: Three proud lobes: the outline breaks now; matches the third picture. Next: a touch broader for an open-grown tree.
- r5: Broader: holds against the third picture. Next: hold; a slightly lower crown base on the second variant.
- r6: Held: form matches the third picture. Next: judge autumn's yellow against the first picture.

- r8: Autumn yellow: matches the yellow of the second picture and close to the first's orange-gold. Next: the far twin.
- r9: Far twins: the oval and the proud lobes survive the coarser facets. Next: hold.
- r10: Scale: 22 and 18 m against 15-30 m -- right. No change this round.

- r18: Second cost pass: 23 bigger lobes, not 30 -- 5,400 to 4,400 triangles. The oval reads the same.

- r23: Autumn: the tall lobed crown in yellow matches the first two pictures' outline -- big masses, a notch or two at the edge, the lower middle mass closing the heart (a small notch left on the second variant reads as an irregular edge, kept). Holds.
- r24: Spring: the tall lobed crown in pale new green; outline holds. 3,426 triangles (round 19: 4,401).
- r25: Spring, second look: tall lobed crown holds.

- r27: Far twins with the far lobes an eighth bigger: the crown closes into the same tall lobed shape as the tree, no floating chunks. Holds.
