# cottonwood: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_lakeshore.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_lakeshore/cottonwood-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: Lineup, as shipped (4,100-5,400 tris): a tall bare pole with a small broccoli crown of clouds pushed to one side; the pictures are a huge broad open crown on a short massive trunk. Next: fork at 0.22 H, four or five great limbs fanning wide (0.3-0.4 H out), a big coarse mass on each plus a side mass, the crown wider than tall with sky between.
- r2: Now a short massive grey trunk fanning into great limbs, crown wider than tall -- the right frame -- but the masses are small clouds on sticks (acacia), not the pictures' heavy full crown. Next: masses a third bigger and taller, the top mass bigger.
- r3: Fuller now: a short grey trunk, great limbs, a broad crown of heavy masses -- but one layer of flat-bottomed pads, too shallow for the pictures' tall broad dome. Next: each limb's side branch climbs to a second tier (0.3 H up), the top mass higher.
- r4: Reads as a cottonwood now: a short massive grey trunk, great limbs fanning out in a V, a broad tall crown of big coarse masses in two tiers, gaps between. Holds; watching it stays apart from the inland oak (longer bare limbs, wider, lighter).
- r5: Holds (3,820-4,620 tris, shipped 4,120-5,410). Its top is a smooth cap; next a fourth lobe on the top mass for the pictures' coarse ragged top.
- r6: Top a little coarser; still a broad crown of big coarse masses on a fan of great grey limbs. Against the pictures the crown is a touch shallow (about 55% of the height, theirs nearer 65%). Next: limb ends and the second tier higher.
- r7: Taller crown, more of the great grey limbs showing in the fan below it: a huge broad open crown of coarse masses on a short massive trunk -- reads as the pictures' cottonwood, apart from the inland oak (longer bare limbs, wider, two tiers) and the elm (no arching vase). Holds.
- r8: Autumn: clear yellow masses on the grey limbs, as cottonwoods turn; outline holds in colour. Holds.
- r9: Far twins (shipped 1,150-1,490): the fan of grey limbs and the broad two-tier crown hold, faceted only. Holds.
- r10: Final, summer, unchanged since round 7: a short massive grey trunk forking low into a fan of great limbs, a huge broad open crown of big coarse masses in two tiers with sky between -- reads as the pictures' eastern cottonwood.
