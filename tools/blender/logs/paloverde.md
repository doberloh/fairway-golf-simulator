# paloverde: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_desert.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_desert/paloverde-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: lineup (round-0 script): a few flat lobe-clumps on bare forked stalks -- reads as a candelabra of broccoli, not the reference's wide see-through umbrella of fine green twigs. Next: rebuild as a fan of green bones (several trunks from the ground, forking twice) ending in fine twigs whose tips sit on one flat dome, the leaves small specks on that dome.
- r2: silhouette 1: now a vase-fan of green bones with an umbrella of specks -- right idea; but the specks are big flat discs (reads as plates) and the colour is washed out. Next: smaller rounder specks, two a twig end; branches as cheap two-segment twigs to pay for them; brighter yellow-green tints; Palo_3 two twigs a branch to stay under its old 3,100.
- r3: silhouette 2: reads as a palo verde fan -- many green stems spreading to a flat-topped umbrella, sky through it -- but the crown is only a thin ring on top and too narrow; the reference's haze reaches down the outer branches and droops at the rim. Next: dome falls off more to the edge, twig ends spread lower, a speck at each branch middle; trunks six-sided and limbs four to pay.
- r4: silhouette 3: now a wide flat-topped umbrella over a fan of green stems, the haze reaching down the outer branches, sky between specks -- reads as palo verde, not ironwood (grey dome) or mesquite (dark shelves). But the leaf went darker (the crown's light centre is too high for the lower haze) and Palo_1 is 14 tris over its old 4,262. Next: crown light centre lower and brighter, eight flowers to seven.
- r5: silhouette 4: brighter yellow-green now (light centre at 0.68 H), wide flat umbrella of specks on a fan of green stems, sky through -- reads as palo verde at thumbnail size; Palo_1 back under budget (4,196 vs 4,262). Palo_3 (two trunks, two twigs a branch) is the sparse one, kept so to stay under its 3,108. Next: specks a little more ragged at the rim.
- r6: silhouette 5: rim specks more ragged; holds -- a wide, flat, see-through umbrella of bright yellow-green specks on a fan of thin green trunks and limbs, the palo verde's outline and nobody else's in the game (ironwood is a grey ragged dome, mesquite dark shelves). Palo_1 4,196, Palo_2 3,884, Palo_3 2,908 tris, all under their old counts.
- r7: silhouette 6 (unchanged geometry, re-rendered): holds -- flat bright umbrella, green bones, sky through. Final summer shape; next the far round.
- r8: far round 1: the far twins cost MORE than the full models (Palo_1_Far 4,460 vs 4,196: the twin rounds three-sided twigs up to four sides, and the specks are already the cheapest lobe). Next: the twin skips the last twigs and draws one bigger speck an end; each end's specks from their own random stream so the twin stays the same tree.
- r9: far round 2: twins now about half (Palo_1_Far 2,408, _2 2,198, _3 1,808) and the same outline at a distance -- the flat bright umbrella on green bones. Final. (The new random streams moved nothing in the near models' shape.)
