# sandcherry: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_drylake.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_drylake/sandcherry-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: six stout limbs each ending in a flat cloud with berries on it -- a bonsai. The pictures show a low sprawl of many thin wand-like stems, narrow leaves and flowers along their upper parts, arching out over the sand. Next: a dozen thin arching stems, leaf sprays along each, flowers or cherries in small lumps along them.
- r2: A dozen thin stems with leaf flakes: a sprawl now, not a bonsai, but sparse and weedy -- big flakes on bare wands. The pictures are a fuller low tangle of many stems with small narrow leaves. Next: sixteen stems, some lying low, smaller and more leaves.
- r3: Sixteen stems, smaller leaves: a sprawl, but each stem's leaves sit in a line like a caterpillar and the cherries dot every stem. The pictures are a loose tangle with a few fruits. Next: leaves scattered off the stem's line, cherries fewer and smaller.
- r4: Leaves scattered off the stem lines, fewer smaller cherries: a loose low tangle that reads like the fourth picture's sprawl over the sand. A little thin against the first picture's full bush. Next: leaves a little bigger.
- r5: Bigger leaves: a fuller low tangle with a few dark cherries, as the sprawl in the fourth picture. Holds. Next: spring, the white flowers.
- r6: Spring: the white flowers are there but pin-pricks, and the leaves are round flakes; the pictures show narrow upward-pointing leaves and white clusters all along the stems. Next: leaves narrow and upright, flowers half as many again and bigger.
- r7: Narrow upward leaves: the leaves now read as the pictures' willow-like ones, the sprawl holds. The white flowers are still too small to show at the sheet's size. Next: flowers about half as big again.
- r8: Bigger flowers: white now dots the stems in spring, a lighter version of the first picture's flowering sprawl. Holds. Next: the far view and summer.
- r9: Summer: the low tangle of thin stems with narrow leaves and a few dark cherries holds against the fourth picture's sprawl. 3,550 triangles, at the shrub budget's top. Next: a cost check.
- r10: Cost: 3,550 triangles, just over a shrub's 3,500. Next: two flowers a stem instead of three, each a little bigger so spring still reads white.
- r11: Autumn: the whole sprawl turns the spec's red (#a83a2e), as the pictures' autumn shrub; two bigger flowers a stem cut it to 3,230 triangles. Holds.
- r12: Winter: drawn in leaf (its bare variant comes with the seasons step), so a colour check only -- holds.
- r13: Summer: the bigger flower lumps from round 11 make the cherries read as plums, too prominent against the pictures' small dark fruit. Next: a size between (4.8 cm), still white enough in spring.
- r14: Spring after the cherries were shrunk: the white flowers nearly vanish -- two a stem is far too few for the pictures' white sprays. Next: four a stem as octahedra (8 triangles each, not 20), so twice the blossoms cost less.
- r15: Four octahedral blossoms a stem (512 triangles, less than the two icosahedra cost): still no white on the sheet -- each sits at its leaf's centre, inside the leaf. Next: each blossom set 7 cm beside its leaf, and a little bigger.
- r16: Blossoms beside the leaves: white flowers now spray along every stem in spring, as the first picture; 3,100 triangles. Next: summer, to see the same lumps as dark cherries.
- r17: Summer, final: a low sprawl of thin dark stems with narrow upward leaves and dark cherries along them; white sprays in spring, red in autumn. 3,100 triangles. Done.
