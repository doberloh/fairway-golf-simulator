# willowthicket: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_mountain.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_mountain/willowthicket-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: 22 red stems with a flat leaf lump each. Big paddles on sticks; the pictures are a dense rounded green mass of fine leaves with the stems hidden. Next: many small leaf lumps filling a mound, stems only at the foot.
- r2: Fourteen stems under a coarse cloud of leaves: one mass now, but it reads as broccoli; the pictures are a fine-textured mound wider than tall. Next: more, smaller lobes, a flatter wider mound, stems lower.
- r3: Twenty-six smaller lobes, a wider mound: still broccoli -- coarse plates on bare red stems. The third picture shows what a thicket is: fine upright leafy shoots packed into a clump. Next: build it from many thin leafy shoots, not lobes.
- r4: Built from fine shoots: the red foot reads, but the whole is a clump of tall grass, upright and spiky, where the pictures are a rounded leafy mound. Next: shoots kept low, many small leaf pieces through a dome above them.
- r5: Shoots low, a leafy dome over them: a leafy mound on red shoots now, close to the third picture. The leaf pieces are big and jagged, the dome stands too high on its legs, and the red is loud; the second picture's mound reaches the grass. Next: the dome lower and down to the ground, smaller pieces, an olive-red foot.
- r6: The dome lowered to the grass, smaller leaf pieces, an olive-red foot: a rounded leafy mound on red shoots, between the first picture's thicket and the third's bush. Next: spring catkins and its cost.
- r7: Spring: the light new green over red shoots, like the third picture; the cost pass, 120 leaf pieces, 3,120 triangles from 3,720. Next: autumn yellow.
- r8: Autumn: yellow over the red foot, as the spec; reads like a willow turning. Next: summer, final shape.
- r9: Summer: grey-green mound on red shoots, 3,120 triangles. Next: winter (bare, red stems: owed to the seasons step).
- r10: Winter: drawn leafy for now -- its bare red stems are owed to the seasons step (PLAN 3). The leafy form holds. Next: finer shoots.
- r11: Held this round. Next: finer shoots at the foot.
- r12: Finer shoots, eleven to a tuft: the foot reads as many thin red stems. Next: the far view and cost.
- r13: Checked as it will be seen, small: the mound reads from its outline; no far twin needed for a 3 m shrub. Next: spring.
- r14: Spring: light new green on the mound over red shoots, close to the third picture. Next: summer, final.
- r15: Summer, final: a rounded grey-green mound on fine red shoots, between the first picture's thicket and the third's bush; 3,390 triangles. Done; its bare winter stems are owed to the seasons step.
