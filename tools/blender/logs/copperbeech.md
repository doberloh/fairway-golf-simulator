# copperbeech: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_neparkland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_neparkland/copperbeech-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a solid dark haystack of big faceted lobes down to the ground, no trunk at all. The pictures (01, 03) are an upright egg-shaped dome on a short visible fork of grey stems, the crown's hem at about a fifth of the height and finely lobed. Silhouette reads as a bush, not the tree. Next: lift the hem, show the stems, many smaller lobes, a taller oval.
- r2: Finer lobes and an egg outline, better; but the hem still hangs to a tenth of the height so no trunk shows, and the dark purple reads as one flat blot. Silhouette: a dome to the ground, not yet the picture's dome on stems. Next: hem at a fifth (first ring at 0.33, no hanging lobes), egg widest at half, a brighter sun side.
- r3: Hem lifted: the short grey trunk now shows under an upright egg dome like 01 and 03 -- the silhouette reads as a big dome on a stem, apart from the American beech's low flat dome. But the hollow underside shows as dark petal-shaped lobes, and the purple is still flat. 4,500 triangles. Next: a ring of flat lobes under the crown round the stems.
- r4: The underside ring closed the hollow: now a full upright dome on a short grey stem, reading as 01-03 at thumbnail and plainly not the American beech's low flat dome. The single tapering stem is thin beside 01's stout fork. Next: a short thick bole forking at a tenth of the height into steep stems that show under the hem.
- r5: Short thick bole and fork now: a dome on a stout grey stem. Silhouette right, but the hem is a hard ring of big lobes where 01-02 have a soft drooping fringe. Next: twelve small hanging lobes round the hem, fewer big lobes to pay for them.
- r6: Hanging fringe round the hem: the lower edge is softer, closer to 01-02; the silhouette is a full upright dome on a short stout stem -- copper beech, not the American beech's low spreading dome. 5,100 triangles. Next: spring colour.
- r7: Spring: deep red-purple (#7a3a3e) new leaf, redder than summer, as 03's young crown. Shape held. Next: autumn.
- r8: Autumn: copper-bronze (#9a5a32) over the whole dome; reads as a beech turning. Shape held. Next: winter.
- r9: Winter: drawn in summer leaf (bare variant later), colour check only. Held.
- r10: Far twins (1,700 triangles) keep the upright dome on its stem; beside the full model the dome shows dark pits between lobes and a notch dividing the top into two humps, where 01-02 are one rounded mass. Next: two big inner masses fill the pits, a bigger cap.
- r11: Inner masses filled the pits and the top notch: one rounded dome now, as 01-02, far twins too. 5,250 triangles, over the 5,000 budget; the fringe hides most of the stem. Next: fewer shell lobes (7 a ring at full width), fringe lifted a little.
- r12: Seven lobes a ring and the fringe lifted: still one full upright dome, the grey stem showing under it as 01; budget back under 5,000. Holds; its silhouette (tall full dome to low on a short stem) is unlike any other tree in the game.
- r13: No change; holds (about 4,800 triangles).
- r14: Cost check: 4,780 triangles (far twin about 1,700). Holds.
- r15: Final, summer: a tall full rounded dome of dark purple-copper leaf, soft hanging hem, on a short stout grey stem -- 01-03's specimen beech; as a black shape a haystack dome on a stub, unlike the American beech's low wide dome on level limbs. 4,870 / 4,790 triangles, far twins 1,610 / 1,570.
