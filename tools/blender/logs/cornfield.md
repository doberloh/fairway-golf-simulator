# cornfield: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_heartland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_heartland/cornfield-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: five rows of bare thin stalks with tiny leaves and fat tassel lumps, see-through. The pictures are a solid wall of arching strap leaves 2.4 m tall with pale tassels over it. Next: six long arching leaves a plant, plants closer in the row, tassels as thin blades.
- r2: Six leaves a plant: still single stalks with short upright leaves, see-through at the sheet's distance; the pictures are a solid wall. Next: longer, wider leaves arching out nearly level, plants closer in the row (0.32 m).
- r3: Longer, wider, more level leaves, plants 0.32 m apart: now a leafy corn patch with tassels, like the first and third pictures from the side; the gaps between rows still show from this angle, which a planted field's next patch will fill. Next: check the colour; tassels hold.
- r4: Held: a leafy patch of corn with pale tassels. Next: spring colours.
- r5: Held. Next: spring colours.
- r6: Spring: lighter green; the same standing corn (the game would plant the patch from mid-summer if it wants young corn). Next: autumn's dry corn.
- r7: Autumn: dry tan corn with pale tassels, like the third picture's late-summer field. Next: winter.
- r8: Winter: pale buff standing stalks; reads as unharvested corn. The game may rather leave the patch out after harvest. Next: cost.
- r9: No far twin. 3,600 triangles for a 4.8 m patch is dear for scenery the game may plant along long field edges. Next: cost -- five leaves a plant, slightly longer, 13 plants a row.
- r10: Five longer leaves a plant, 13 plants a row 0.37 m apart: 2,730 triangles from 3,600, and the patch still reads as a leafy wall of corn with tassels. Next: hold.
