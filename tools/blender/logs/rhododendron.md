# rhododendron: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_appalachia.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_appalachia/rhododendron-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First version: a mound of crooked stems ending in starbursts of long drooping leaves, pink trusses on top. The starbursts read as rhododendron, but the mass sits up on bare stems; the pictures are dense to the ground and wider. Next: more, lower rosettes (a skirt), a wider lower mound, more trusses.
- r2: The skirt of low rosettes made it a wide dense mound to the ground like pictures 01 and 03, the starburst leaf edge reading as rhododendron. Holds; next a little more height variation along the top so it is not one even dome.
- r3: Wide dense mound with a starburst leaf edge and trusses on top, as pictures 01/03. Holds.
- r4: Holds: wide dense starburst-edged mound, trusses on top. Next: spring round to check the leaf colour without trusses.
- r5: Spring: no trusses, the dark evergreen mound with its starburst edge reads alone. Holds.
- r6: Autumn: unchanged evergreen mound, as the spec. Holds.
- r7: Far round: no far twin (a shrub). Counted 4,940 triangles, over the shrub budget, 1,940 of it hidden stems. Next (cost): stems as cheap two-segment twigs.
- r8: Cost round: cheap twig stems took it from 4,940 to 3,900 triangles, the mound and starburst edge unchanged. Next: six leaves a rosette and cheaper trusses, to come under 3,500.
- r9: Winter: #3a5c32, the mound a shade darker as the spec (leaves curled in cold, colour only). Six-leaf rosettes and cheaper trusses still read. Holds.
- r10: Summer: wide dense mound, starburst leaf edge, pale pink-mauve trusses over the top as pictures 01/03. Holds.
- r11: Holds.
- r12: Holds.
- r13: Spring again: dark evergreen mound, no trusses, holds.
- r14: Spring: holds.
- r15: Second cost count: 3,480 triangles, inside the shrub budget. Holds.
- r16: Final (summer): wide dense mound to the ground, crooked stems, starbursts of long drooping leaves on every tip, pale mauve-pink trusses on top; reads as a rosebay thicket and apart from the laurel. 3,480 triangles. Ships.
