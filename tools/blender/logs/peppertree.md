# peppertree: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_calinland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_calinland/peppertree-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: weeping curtains on arching limbs -- already reads as a weeper, but the strands are fat flat slabs and the top is thin. Next: thinner, more numerous strands, a fuller domed top.
- r2: Weeping read holds; strands are regular green columns and the top dome is thin. Next: bigger top clumps, strands begun inside them, lengths varied, a slight outward lean.
- r3: Fuller top, varied strands: reads as a weeping pepper beside the pictures. Holds; strands may be thinned in the cost round.
- r4: Holds: weeping curtains from arching limbs, a gnarled trunk.
- r5: Holds.
- r6: Spring: yellow-green weeping curtains as in the first picture; holds.
- r7: Spring again: holds.
- r8: Autumn: the pink peppercorns barely show among the strands. Next: berry clusters bigger and on half the strands.
- r9: Winter: the bigger pink peppercorn clusters show now among the strands, as the spec asks.
- r10: Far round (no twin): 5,430 triangles. Next: two bigger top clumps a limb instead of three.
- r11: Cost round: 4,710 with two top clumps a limb; weeping curtains, pink berries; holds.
- r12: Summer recheck: holds.
- r13: Spring: the strands still read as broad planks; the pictures are fine hair. Next: four thinner strands at each point, cheaper balls.
- r14: Four thin strands a point: the curtains now read as fine hanging hair, closer to the pictures, with pink berries in them; 5,050 triangles, at the top of a tree's budget. Silhouette: a willow-like weeper on a gnarled trunk -- the only weeping tree of the pack apart from the valley oak's hem.
- r15: Far round (no twin): fine hanging curtains with pink berries; holds.
- r16: Summer, final: a gnarled leaning trunk, limbs arching up and over, curtains of fine strands hanging almost to the grass, pink berry clusters; 5,050 triangles. Done.
