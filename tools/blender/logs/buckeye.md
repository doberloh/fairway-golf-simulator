# buckeye: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_calinland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_calinland/buckeye-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a brown haystack sitting on the ground, no stems seen, candles like sticks. The pictures show smooth silver stems splaying under a broad dome. Next: lift the crown so the stems show, lower ring smaller, upper wider.
- r2: The crown lifted: a broad mound on splayed silver stems, close to the third picture; tan summer colour as the spec says. Next: a little wider than tall, candles shorter.
- r3: Broad mound on silver stems, wider than tall; candles shorter. Holds; next check in spring for the white candles.
- r4: Holds: broad mound, wider than tall, silver stems under it.
- r5: Holds. Next: spring, for the candles.
- r6: Spring: the white candles are only dots, where the third picture is covered in them. Next: bigger candles, five a clump, standing proud of the skin.
- r7: Bigger candles show now as white dashes, but the third picture's plumes are long and cover the crown. Next: candles half a metre, six a clump.
- r8: Autumn: the long candles in the pod colour stand up as brown spikes -- wrong, pods hang and the tree is bare then (its bare variant comes later). Decided: no accent outside spring and summer (drawn the leaf colour).
- r9: Winter: the candles in leaf colour stand up as green spikes all over the mound -- they are geometry all year. Next: candles shorter and sunk a little, so off-season they read as leafy tufts.
- r10: Far round (no twin): 4,280 triangles, 1,560 of them candles. Next: three candles a clump.
- r11: Cost round: 3,500 triangles with three candles a clump; the faded summer candles still show. Holds.
- r12: Summer recheck: holds.
- r13: Spring after the cost round: three candles a clump are too few beside the third picture's crown covered in plumes. Next: five a clump, each a cheaper 16-triangle spindle.
- r14: Five 16-triangle candles a clump: 3,760 triangles, the crown dotted with plumes again; faded tan in summer. Holds.
- r15: Far round (no twin): the low broad mound on silver stems, plumes dotted over it; holds.
- r16: Summer, final: a low broad mound, wider than tall, on splayed silver stems, candle plumes over it (tan in summer, white in spring); 3,760 triangles. Done; bare late-summer/autumn variant owed.
