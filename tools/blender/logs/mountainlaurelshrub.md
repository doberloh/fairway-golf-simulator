# mountainlaurelshrub: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_appalachia.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_appalachia/mountainlaurelshrub-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First version: gnarled stems under three flat umbrella plates -- reads as a little tree, not the dense billowy rounded shrub of the pictures (whole mass under flowers). Next: more stems and ends, small tufts at many heights filling a rounded mound, flowers spread over it.
- r2: Still a small tree: tufts sit high on bare stems with a lollipop on top. Pictures are a rounded billowy mass from near the ground. Next: tufts placed on a dome shell from 0.2 of the height, a low skirt, no tuft above the mound.
- r3: Skirt and core made it the rounded mass of the pictures, low to the ground; but the tufts are coarse faceted blocks and too dark. Next: rounder finer tufts (8 x 4), a touch brighter.
- r4: Rounder finer tufts and brighter: a dense rounded billowy mass like the pictures; summer shows no flowers (spec: May-Jun, drawn in spring). Next: spring round for the pink-white cups.
- r5: Spring: the pink-white cups show but as a sparse scatter; pictures 02/03/17 are smothered. Next: bigger flower domes (0.38 m) so they cover the tops of the tufts.
- r6: Autumn: evergreen, no flowers; the rounded billowy mound holds.
- r7: Far round: no twin. Counted 5,120 triangles, over budget (stems 1,215, flowers 1,380). Next (cost): cheap stems, two bigger flower domes an end instead of three.
- r8: Cost round: 5,120 to 3,870 triangles; but capping the tufts made a flat table top, where the pictures are domed. Next: cap at 0.9 of the height and one core tuft high in the middle, so the top rounds.
- r9: Winter #3a5c32: colour holds. The high core tuft rounded the top somewhat; still a little square-shouldered against the pictures' domes. Next: core tufts raised to 0.55-0.75 of the height.
- r10: Raised core tufts: a rounded dense evergreen mass on short gnarled stems; reads as a laurel and apart from the rhododendron's starbursts (finer, rounder tufts, no long leaves). Holds.
- r11: Holds.
- r12: Holds in summer; the bigger flower domes (r5) not yet seen in spring. Next: spring round.
- r13: Spring: bigger pink-white domes now read as laurel bloom over the outer tufts, but the top middle stays bare green where the pictures are covered. Next: two flower domes on each core tuft too.
- r14: Spring: flowers on the core tufts too -- the rounded mound is now covered in pink-white cups over the top, as pictures 02/03/17. Reads as laurel in bloom. Holds; count triangles next.
- r15: Second cost count: the core flowers took it to 4,210, over the 3,500 shrub budget. Next: one tuft on each skirt end, seven-sided tufts, cheaper 16-triangle flower domes.
- r16: Final (summer): rounded billowy evergreen mass of fine tufts on short gnarled stems; flowers over the whole top in spring (r14). Cost cut to 3,350 triangles with the shape unchanged; the spring look at this cost not re-rendered. Ships.
