# pitchpine: iterations (tools/blender/cartoon_neparkland.py)

One line a round: what was wrong beside the reference pictures, and what changed. The side-by-side of each round: bench/shots/blender/iter/cartoon_neparkland/pitchpine-r<round>.png (local).

- r1: First build: a crooked stick with a few small flat pads -- a bonsai, nothing like 01's broad bushy crown or 03's crooked trunk under a full irregular head. Tufts too few, too small, too flat; limbs too short. Next: wider spread, more and longer limbs, clusters of 4-5 rounder tufts along each.
- r2: Fuller, broader crown on a crooked trunk now, like 03's shape -- but the big smooth clumps read as a cartoon oak, not a pine. Next: smaller tufts with needle spikes radiating from each, so the edge bristles.
- r3: Needle spikes on each tuft: the edge now bristles and no longer reads as an oak, more a cartoon pine; still narrow (a column) where 01 and 03 are broad irregular heads, and the spikes cost 7,180 triangles. Next: longer upper limbs for a broader head, three spiky tufts a limb end and one part-way, six spikes a tuft, none on the far twin.
- r4: Broader crown on the crooked trunk, gaps between tufts like 03; triangles down. The short fat spikes still make each tuft a holly leaf. Next: seven thin longer needles a tuft, tilted up; a cheaper core; _2 broader (11 m spread on 11 m, 01's bushy shape).
- r5: Thin needles tilted up: each tuft is now a brush of needles, reading as a cartoon pine tuft, nothing like an oak lobe; crooked trunk, gappy head as 03. Still sparser than 01. Next: bigger tufts, two part-way along each limb; cheaper bark.
- r6: Fuller head: _2 is now 01's broad bushy pine on a crooked trunk, _1 the open gnarled tree of 03-04; needle brushes keep both apart from any oak. 4,800 triangles. Next: spring.
- r7: Spring: yellow-green new growth (#6e8a46); both variants held. Next: autumn.
- r8: Autumn: evergreen, unchanged (#647e40). Held.
- r9: Winter: slightly darker evergreen (#607a3e). Held.
- r10: Far twin of _1 (2,000 triangles) drops the needles, so its tufts become plain hexagon pads -- still a crooked open pine at a distance, but the bristle is gone. Next: two needles a tuft on the far twin, no trunk tufts there.
- r11: Far twin now keeps two needles a tuft: still bristly at a distance, 2,820 triangles against 4,790 (the bark is most of it). Accepted.
- r12: No change; holds -- crooked trunk, open head of bristly needle tufts (_1 03-04, _2 01).
- r13: No change; holds.
- r14: Cost check: 4,790 triangles, far twin of _1 2,820. Holds.
- r15: Final, summer: a dark crooked kinking trunk with tufts sprouting from it, crooked limbs ending in bristly needle-brush tufts with sky between; _1 tall open and gnarled (03-04), _2 low broad and bushy (01). Reads as neither the white pine's shelves nor an oak. 4,790 triangles, far twin 2,820.
