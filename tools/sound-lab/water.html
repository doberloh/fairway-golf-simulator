Lapping water

Built on branch sounds. Options for the pond, lake, river and creek sound. The sea is not part of this.

Each card plays its water continuously: small waves arriving at the bank and drawing back, on an uneven rhythm that rises and falls in sets over half a minute. Only one plays at a time. Distance is how far the ball is from the water in the game, and Wind makes the waves bigger and quicker. The last two cards are what plays in the game today, for comparison. Pick one, nudge it under Adjust if you like, and press Copy my notes.
