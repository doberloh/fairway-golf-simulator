Water sounds

Built on branch sounds. Round 6: ponds and lakes, and separately rivers and creeks. The sea is not part of this.

Round 8: all the water is signed off and in the game. The bubbly lap for ponds and lakes, the mountain stream for rivers, the babbling creek for creeks, each with your sliders built in. In a round the game plays whichever is nearest the ball, louder the nearer it is, from the side it is on; the sea keeps its surf.

Each card plays continuously; one at a time. Distance is how far the ball is from the water. Wind moves the pond's waves, Flow the rivers.
