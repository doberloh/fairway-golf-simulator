Swing Meter sounds

Built on branch swing-meter. Round 1. None of these play in the game yet.

What this is. Sounds for the Swing Meter: a voice that follows the marker as it climbs and comes back, and one sound for each thing a swing can do. Every one has three takes to choose between, plus sliders. Pick a take on each card, nudge the sliders if you like, and write anything in the notes box.

Try them together in the practice meter just below: it uses whichever takes you have picked. Space, a click or a tap on the bar: start, set the distance, strike on the white line. It judges like the game on Normal.

When you are done, press Copy my notes and paste it back to me. Everything you set is kept in this browser if you come back.
