XP sounds

Built on branch swing-meter. Round 1 signed off: each is the take you chose with your sliders built in, and they play in the game.

What this is. Whenever a golfer earns XP a popup comes up with what earned it and their level bar filling. Three sounds go with it: the gain as it comes up, the fill under the bar, and a new level when the bar runs over the top. Each has three takes and sliders.

Try them together with the demo just below, which plays the popup the way the game does with the takes you have picked. The order is the game's: the hole's own score sting first (the chiptune jingle you chose), and the popup only once that has finished; then the gain, a beat, the bar filling; and on a level-up the bar stops at the top, the level-up sound plays by itself, and only then does the bar fill on.
