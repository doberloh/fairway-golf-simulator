Hole-out stings, chiptune

Built on branch swing-meter. Round 1. The game still plays the marimba stings until you pick.

What this is. The short tune when a hole is finished -- one for each score from a hole in one to a triple bogey or worse -- reworked as chiptune, the old game-console sound: square and thin pulse waves, a triangle bass, a noise drum, quick and dry. Four takes. A, B and C play the tunes you already know on chip voices; D is new, bouncier game jingles.

Pick one take for all of them at the top, so the set sounds like one game, and nudge the sliders. If one score wants a different take, change it on its card. Current on each card plays the marimba you have now, to compare. Play them all runs from the worst to the best. When you are done, Copy my notes and paste it back.
